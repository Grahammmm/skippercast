# Lopez Point source package needed before a 300 ft fishing-area rank

This is a **prepared data request**, not a sent message. It concerns the Lopez Point 2010 products in NOAA NCEI's [Big Creek additional-products archive](https://data.ngdc.noaa.gov/platforms/ocean/ships/ventresca/BigCreek/multibeam/data/version2/products/BigCreek_additional_products.tar.gz). The original 2 m/5 m DEM and derived habitat grids are NAVD88 Geoid09; the related 1 m Reson and SWATHplus backscatter metadata explicitly name NAD83(CORS96) for those **backscatter** mosaics. The backscatter statement does not resolve the final bathymetry frame. The [cell/ROV audit](../research/receipts/bigcreek-lopez-rov-original-cell-join.json) found historical fish observations in the nominal 200–300 ft source band, but its rough-class association is unstable over 10 m, and the [backscatter audit](../research/receipts/lopez-original-backscatter-source-review.json) found no declared valid-data mask or cleared reuse rights. Neither result is a fishing mark.

## Requested source package

Please identify the actual contributing survey lines and dates for the Lopez Point 2 m and 5 m final grids, and provide a bounded extract covering the open-reference Lopez area, ideally with:

1. Original CARIS CUBE/BASE or equivalent node table with elevation, total propagated vertical uncertainty or defensible upper bound, sounding density, valid-node mask, hypothesis selection and source-line lineage. Please distinguish measured nodes from filled/interpolated DEM pixels and explain the published 80–85 m overlap between the 2 m and 5 m products.
2. Exact horizontal realization and coordinate epoch of the **final bathymetry grid**; source and target vertical frames, Geoid09 version, SBET/tide reduction, and a documented path to chart MLLW. Include line-specific positioning RMS, checkline results, and a conservative horizontal error model suitable for comparing separate surveys.
3. The native valid-data/nodata masks and intensity definitions for both `LopezPoint_7125_1m_SSS.tif` and `LopezPoint_SWATH_1m_SSS.tif`; radiometric processing parameters, mosaic seam/priority masks and the registration test between each backscatter mosaic and the final depth grid. State whether amplitude values are comparable between lines or instruments.
4. Independent bottom observations (camera, ROV, grab or interpreted geology) with original bottom positions, camera/ship offset, achieved positional uncertainty, substrate annotations, observation dates, sampling effort and negative/soft-bottom samples. Identify observations used to train the published rough/smooth habitat class so they can be excluded from held-out validation. The public [2024 Zenodo ROV release](https://zenodo.org/records/10929417) has historical 10 m subunit averages but does not include an achieved bottom-position error bound for this join.
5. Written access and reuse terms for a public, potentially commercial fishing-planning site: whether we may compute and publish **derived, generalized** habitat summaries and representative images without redistributing raw mosaics or disclosing sensitive survey/camera positions. The archived product metadata leave access and use constraints to be determined, and [CSUMB's current library policy](https://csumb.edu/undersea/sfml-data-library/) calls for express permission for for-profit use.

A link to a producer-approved subset is preferable to transferring a complete raw survey. A response that only repeats grid spacing, nominal ± values that “vary with depth,” or a datum for **another** product will remain a source lead; it will not clear a rank.

## Acceptance and integration

Pin every supplied file and license, preserve native masks and CRS, and record the survey-to-grid lineage. Convert each candidate's **entire** depth footprint plus a reviewed horizontal margin to MLLW, combine the survey and transformation uncertainty conservatively, and require its deepest plausible cell to remain no deeper than 300 ft MLLW. Compare separate acoustic and visual bottom evidence at the uncertainty-supported scale, including soft-bottom negatives and held-out transects. Then screen the complete approach, drift and return footprints against current CDFW MPAs, federal/security restrictions and chart hazards. Only after those gates, a source-rights review and dated local rules review may a human assign a 1–3 habitat-fit rank. Fish observations remain historical support, not catch odds.
