For B or C, price a **display-only** change. The proposed behavior and its browser/print results are **UNVERIFIED**:

- **Same contours, different labels.** Keep the foot-based geometry: the older lines are every 30 ft with heavier lines every 150 ft; newer lines are tiered by zoom, not a single metric interval. For example, a 30 ft line can read `9.1 m`; the key should say that metric labels are conversions of foot contours, not lines newly drawn every 9 m or 10 m. Convert the raw values before rounding; use one decimal in metres for the converted depths and range ends, and use the original foot values for selection and screening. (`web/map/depth-contours.ts:53–72,241–258,303–309,359–379`; `web/units.ts:3–4`; `web/ranking.ts:120–123,361–369`.) Round-metre contours would be different work, with a change to the data contract; cost **UNVERIFIED**, not included.
- **Same survey reference.** The datum is the survey's zero level. Keep its name or the warning that it is unknown, along with “nominal,” “estimated,” “not a sounding,” and “not for navigation” wherever they apply. Changing the unit does not move that zero, adjust for the tide, or improve accuracy. Keep actual survey grid sizes and uncertainty in their existing units. (`web/map/depth-contours.ts:328–379`; `web/map/seafloor.ts:113–122,275–281`; `web/confidence.ts:75–87`; `web/disclaimers.ts:29–35`.)
- **Print the chosen depth unit**, with the unit attached to every depth or range. Keep the current wave/tide print convention separate: tiles can show metres on screen but feet on paper. Under A, neither print path changes. Under B or C, keep downloadable trip HTML and GPX as clearly marked feet, and leave fixed source notes, official rule wording and feet-only input controls unchanged. Display the chosen unit for read-only limits such as the Brief's Spear button, not for the underlying editor; the unit is already named beside the editor. (`web/brief/Tiles.tsx:53–60`; `web/brief/brief-print.css:12–39`; `dist/trip-export.js:119–120,211`; `web/app/Planner.tsx:275`; `web/app/LimitsPanel.tsx:137–140`; `web/regs.ts:184–190`.)
