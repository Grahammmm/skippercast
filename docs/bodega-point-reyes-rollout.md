# Bodega Bay–Point Reyes preview

This is an outer-coast **source and conditions preview**, not a fishing-spot or navigation release. The 38°02′ N salmon boundary splits the discovery extent. The northern local area hides salmon because the reviewed 2026 September–October opening does not establish an opening north of that line. San Francisco Bay is outside the package.

The [San Francisco source review](../jurisdictions/reviews/2026-09-24-california-san-francisco.json) binds the separate management-area rules, the CDFW area and Cordell notices, and the Bodega/Point Reyes local contexts. The preview uses NWS PZZ540/PZZ570 for Bodega and PZZ545/PZZ571 for Point Reyes; NDBC 46013 is an offshore observation, not a Bodega Harbor entrance sensor. NOAA 9415625 and 9415020 provide predictions, not live entrance currents. The [CDFW San Francisco region](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Fishing-Map/San-Francisco) remains the primary legal check.

A fresh September 24 local rehearsal of the normal daily collector returned SST, chlorophyll, both independent wind models, both independent wave models, buoy and swell observations, tides, marine alerts, MPA boundaries and all watched rule sources. Surface-radar currents had no usable vectors and are shown as missing. The live collector and dated habitat collector also completed locally with the Bodega offshore buoy as an explicit verification reference. The optional GEFS wave-probability decoder failed in the local GIS environment because `eccodes` was absent; the hosted workflow installs it. These `var/review/` receipts do **not** establish a successful public scheduled feed; check its first published regional receipts.

The package deliberately publishes **zero fishing targets, zero search footprints, zero drift lines and no surveyed bottom images**. Seven original NOAA/USGS hard-bottom source reviews remain historical context. A present chart and harbor approach, exact original-cell footprint, current MPA and Cordell exclusions, species/date/method rules, and site-level habitat evidence must pass before a target or export is added. A general harbor page, tide prediction or regional buoy cannot clear the Bodega entrance.

The [original-cell terrain queue](../dist/data/bodega-native-hard-terrain-review.json) now reads the exact cached NOAA BAG named by every one of the 74 original-grid context outlines intersecting the preview. It applies the same uncalibrated relief, roughness and hard-area rubric used for qualified Southern patches, while requiring every displayed raster cell to pass the original MLLW depth/uncertainty screen. **Eighteen outlines** reach the minimum 2,500 m² display size; all 18 grade C (best score 53/100), and 56 eroded fragments are too small to rank. One ranked outline is near selected current charted-danger records. This is a review priority list, not fishing positions or a catch probability. No A/B-grade Bodega patch emerged from this bounded original-grid set; that does not prove there are no better reefs elsewhere. The three ENC danger scopes are now rechecked by the monthly substrate job, with unpublished receipts for human review before any public source promotion.

The [three-scope NOAA ENC research receipt](../dist/data/bodega-native-enc-scope-review.json) now covers the complete displayed footprint of all **74** original-grid rock-context outlines intersecting this regional package. It pins each raw query's digest, timestamp and 18 selected danger-layer counts. One outline is within a 100 m buffer of a charted danger record; the other 73 have no nearby record in those selected layers. This is a chart cross-check only: ENC Direct is not a certified chart, and a zero proximity hit cannot clear the harbor approach, route or site. The original 2007–2009 surveys, present closures, method rules and fish evidence still prevent every outline from becoming a fishing target. Rerun the two new bounded research scopes with `scripts/refresh_enc_hazards.py --scope bodega-original-north --output var/review/bodega-original-north-enc.geojson` and its `reyes-original-south` counterpart; refresh `point-reyes-tomales-enc-current.geojson` the same way, then run `scripts/audit_bodega_native_enc_scope.py`. The script rejects stale or partial source receipts.
