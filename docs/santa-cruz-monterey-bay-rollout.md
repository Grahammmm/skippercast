# Pigeon Point–Monterey Bay rollout

`santa-cruz-monterey-bay` is a limited regional preview for the 36.6°–37°11′ N discovery band. Its Santa Cruz Harbor origin, three weather samples and bounded MPA snapshot are local to this band. The public map has **no qualified fishing points, areas, drift lines, ranks, bottom images or GPX fishing exports** for this package. The browse rectangle is neither a legal line nor a fishing position.

## Original bottom and promotion hold

- [USGS/CSUMB Offshore Monterey original grids](../catalog/candidates/usgs-offshore-monterey-original-grids.json) include a hash-pinned 2 m seafloor-class raster and bathymetry raster. The class code 3 represents rugose rock/boulder, and the [native-source review](../dist/data/usgs-offshore-monterey-native-audit.json) establishes that the bathymetry is NAVD88 rather than the chart's MLLW datum. The original grids do not include a paired per-cell uncertainty product. The 88 generalized [research outlines](../dist/data/usgs-offshore-monterey-hard-context.geojson) overlap five historical camera-observation groups; neither count represents 88 fishing sites or present fish.
- Five [NOAA VDatum point probes](../dist/data/noaa-vdatum-offshore-monterey-samples.json) returned an offset and conversion uncertainty, while a sixth returned a missing sentinel. They do not convert every grid pixel, establish the source's exact horizontal realization, or supply missing product uncertainty. A target still needs a bounded measured-cell depth and substrate subset, conservative datum/uncertainty screen, current charted dangers, complete exclusion geometry, route and legal review. Until then the bottom layer remains research context, outside ratings and export.

## Local MPAs, rules and conditions

- The [official CDFW polygon query](../dist/regions/santa-cruz-monterey-bay/protected-areas.geojson) returned **12** named protected areas within the bounded envelope, including Año Nuevo, Greyhound Rock, Soquel Canyon and Portuguese Ledge. The [Año Nuevo/Greyhound Rock](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Ano-Nuevo-Greyhound-Rock), [Soquel Canyon](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Soquel-Canyon) and [Portuguese Ledge](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Portuguese-Ledge) rules have different exceptions. SkipperCast excludes every mapped MPA from fishing marks and exports even when a method exception might exist. The exact local legal pages and the [CDFW Central fishing rules](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Fishing-Map/Central) have a [hash-bound review](../jurisdictions/reviews/2026-09-25-california-central-santa-cruz-monterey-bay.json). Vandenberg maritime access is held after HTTP 403 and affects its own area; regional collection now watches only applicable species and notice sources.
- [NWS Monterey Bay PZZ535](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?TextType=2&zoneid=PZZ535), [PZZ560](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?TextType=2&zoneid=PZZ560) and offshore PZZ575 cover different portions of this preview. [NDBC 46236](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=46236) is a Monterey Canyon Outer wave/current-profile point in about 133 m water; [46042](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=46042) is far offshore in about 1,710 m. Neither clears the Santa Cruz bar or a shallow reef. [NOAA Santa Cruz 9413745](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/noaatideannual.html?id=9413745) is a subordinate tide-prediction station, not a bar-current sensor.
- The first local rehearsal fetched both wind models, both wave models, the two buoy references, all three NWS zones, tide predictions, 12 CDFW polygons, SST, chlorophyll and surface radar. HFR returned **138 populated of 228** requested surface cells; the SST request had **185 of 288** populated cells; chlorophyll had only **33 of 512**. These are dated surface context, not bottom flow or fish observations. [Santa Cruz Port District](https://santacruzharbor.specialdistrict.org/) posts harbor information and a webcam, while the [NOAA Coast Pilot](https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/publications/coast-pilot/files/cp7/CPB7_C06_WEB.pdf) warns of entrance shoaling. A fetched page or predicted tide does not establish departure or return clearance.

## Next qualification gates

1. Reopen the exact USGS/CSUMB class and bathymetry rasters. Transform qualified cells to MLLW with the proper source realization and a conservative per-cell total uncertainty; reject cells with unknown datum or uncertainty.
2. Intersect original hard class, measured depth, dated camera support and exact current CDFW/federal exclusion geometry. Check NOAA ENC hazards and a real harbor-to-ground route before producing any target polygon.
3. Retest legal, entrance and source freshness on the scheduled feeds. Keep 6 km surface current distinct from bottom drift and mark cloud-masked ocean-color cells unavailable.
4. Only then add species-specific, ranked fishing geometry and chartplotter exports with source/provenance notes. Verify mobile map behavior and no export inside an MPA.
