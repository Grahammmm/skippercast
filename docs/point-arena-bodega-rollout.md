# Point Arena to northern Sonoma rollout

This preview adds the northern portion of the statewide `arena-bodega` discovery sector. Its exact fishing envelope is 38.45–38.9583° N; the neighboring Bodega–Point Reyes package covers the coast south of 38.45° N. The stable package ID is `point-arena-bodega`, but its displayed name describes its present coverage. Bodega Harbor is only a distant planning origin, not a Point Arena departure assumption or safe transit plan.

The package uses NWS PZZ540/PZZ570, Arena Cove and Bodega Harbor water-level references, NDBC 46013/46014 offshore observations, and three separate forecast sample points. The first regional daily collection on September 25, 2026 returned 67/67 configured source checks, including GFS/IFS wind and GFS Wave/WAM seas. That receipt confirms access on that run, not future availability, harbor clearance or a seven-day forecast verification. The shared daily and live workflows discover this non-draft package automatically; there is no separate scheduler.

The bounded [CDFW MPA snapshot](../dist/regions/point-arena-bodega/protected-areas.geojson) contains 11 polygons. Six local [official CDFW MPA pages](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs) were added to the San Francisco jurisdiction's hash-bound legal review, giving 30 reviewed sources. Their rules differ: Point Arena SMR is no-take while its SMCA permits salmon trolling only, Sea Lion Cove permits finfish, Saunders Reef permits salmon trolling, Stewarts Point and Salt Point have method-specific exceptions, and Russian River has crab/surf-smelt exceptions. The app conservatively excludes **all** mapped MPA polygons from fishing marks and exports; an exception in one area does not turn it into a general fishing target. The reviewed fall 2026 salmon opening starts at 38°02′ N, south of this package, so its salmon selector is hidden during that reviewed season.

The original NOAA/USGS intersection has **56** H11732/H11733 historical outlines inside this package, clustered near Fort Ross–Salt Point. A fresh bounded NOAA ENC Direct review checked all 18 selected danger layers and found one outline within 100 m of charted dangers. Historical survey rocks remain unreconciled with the current chart. A fresh NOAA federal-area pull supplied all 29 expected areas; the exact NOAA BAGs matched pinned SHA-256 values and their 2 m MLLW cells were sampled inside every outline. Nineteen larger outlines passed the research-only terrain/depth screen; 36 were too small and one was held for charted-danger proximity. The [dated original-cell audit](../dist/data/arena-native-hard-terrain-review.json) and [map research layer](../dist/regions/point-arena-bodega/survey-habitat.geojson) record their sampled depth range and relief, with no calibrated fish quality grade.

There are still **zero** qualified fishing marks, drifts, chartplotter exports or bottom renderings in this preview. The displayed outlines are inset historical source context, not individual boulders or promises of fish. To publish a location, reconcile the original survey dangers against the current certified chart, verify approach and harbor conditions, exact date/method legal access, source rights and independent species evidence. A regional forecast or nearby buoy does not supply those missing gates.

The reproducible review commands are:

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/refresh_enc_hazards.py --scope h11732-h11733-fort-ross-salt-point-context --output var/review/enc-hazards-h11732-h11733-fort-ross-salt-point.geojson
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/collect_noaa_groundfish_areas.py --output var/review/noaa-federal-areas.json
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/audit_regional_native_hard_terrain.py --region point-arena-bodega --enc var/review/enc-hazards-h11732-h11733-fort-ross-salt-point.geojson --federal var/review/noaa-federal-areas.json --output dist/data/arena-native-hard-terrain-review.json
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/build_regional_native_hard_preview.py --region point-arena-bodega --audit dist/data/arena-native-hard-terrain-review.json --enc var/review/enc-hazards-h11732-h11733-fort-ross-salt-point.geojson --federal var/review/noaa-federal-areas.json --output dist/regions/point-arena-bodega/survey-habitat.geojson
```

The two review scripts require fresh bounded CDFW MPA, federal and ENC snapshots plus the exact cached original BAG files. The monthly source workflow rechecks the bounded ENC screen; any source or geometry change requires a fresh reviewed publication, never an automatic fishing-target promotion.

Rebuild with `PYTHONPATH=src python -m skippercast.platform.build`, then run the shared regional daily and live collectors. A change to any watched legal source must go through the explicit decision process in [regulations.md](regulations.md); an unchanged retrieval cannot clear an exact trip position. Roll back by restoring the prior coherent package and site version.
