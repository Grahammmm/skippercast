# Point Arena to northern Sonoma rollout

This preview adds the northern portion of the statewide `arena-bodega` discovery sector. Its exact fishing envelope is 38.45–38.9583° N; the neighboring Bodega–Point Reyes package covers the coast south of 38.45° N. The stable package ID is `point-arena-bodega`, but its displayed name describes its present coverage. Bodega Harbor is only a distant planning origin, not a Point Arena departure assumption or safe transit plan.

The package uses NWS PZZ540/PZZ570, Arena Cove and Bodega Harbor water-level references, NDBC 46013/46014 offshore observations, and three separate forecast sample points. The first regional daily collection on September 25, 2026 returned 67/67 configured source checks, including GFS/IFS wind and GFS Wave/WAM seas. That receipt confirms access on that run, not future availability, harbor clearance or a seven-day forecast verification. The shared daily and live workflows discover this non-draft package automatically; there is no separate scheduler.

The bounded [CDFW MPA snapshot](../dist/regions/point-arena-bodega/protected-areas.geojson) contains 11 polygons. Six local [official CDFW MPA pages](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs) were added to the San Francisco jurisdiction's hash-bound legal review, giving 30 reviewed sources. Their rules differ: Point Arena SMR is no-take while its SMCA permits salmon trolling only, Sea Lion Cove permits finfish, Saunders Reef permits salmon trolling, Stewarts Point and Salt Point have method-specific exceptions, and Russian River has crab/surf-smelt exceptions. The app conservatively excludes **all** mapped MPA polygons from fishing marks and exports; an exception in one area does not turn it into a general fishing target. The reviewed fall 2026 salmon opening starts at 38°02′ N, south of this package, so its salmon selector is hidden during that reviewed season.

The original NOAA/USGS hard-bottom intersection offers historical research leads here, but there are **zero** qualified fishing marks, areas, drifts, chartplotter exports or bottom renderings in this preview. To publish a location, review the original BAG depth cells and datum, native substrate footprint and age, current charted dangers, full MPA and other access geometry, route/harbor conditions and species evidence. A regional forecast or nearby buoy does not supply any of those missing gates.

Rebuild with `PYTHONPATH=src python -m skippercast.platform.build`, then run the shared regional daily and live collectors. A change to any watched legal source must go through the explicit decision process in [regulations.md](regulations.md); an unchanged retrieval cannot clear an exact trip position. Roll back by restoring the prior coherent package and site version.
