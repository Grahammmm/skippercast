# Monterey 2009 original survey: source acquisition brief

**Status:** prepared research request; it has not been sent. This is a source-acquisition brief, not a fishing-spot release.

The [NCEI CentralMontereyBay cruise](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/ships/ventresca/CentralMontereyBay_mb.html) identifies CSU Monterey Bay Seafloor Mapping Lab as source organization and the Ventresca survey dates as September 3–November 12, 2009. The [per-file index](https://gis.ngdc.noaa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/multibeam_files/MapServer/0) intersects five processed Reson 7125 GSF files with priority Monterey research outlines `023` and `046`. The [beam audit](../dist/data/monterey-2009-centralmontereybay-valid-beam-review.json) finds 314,362 and 72,083 valid processed soundings within those outlines, respectively. The [nearest-cell audit](../dist/data/monterey-2009-beam-usgs-cell-screen.json) matches them to original USGS 2 m bathymetry and interpreted seafloor character, but cannot establish independent evidence because the USGS bathymetry may reuse these lines. The GSF audit excludes ignored pings and invalid beam flags. Its source processing record reads `TIDE_COMPENSATED=YES` and `TIDAL_DATUM=UNKNOWN`.

The linked **MontPen_BigSur__Tides_ancillary.tar.gz** has SHA-256 `ed83dbee1a3971f68725d6a5fd5d80939a83a41b6bf95ae37f66ae416c312f32`. Its 44 files have names indicating 2000–2006 tide sets; none names a 2009 CentralMontereyBay line. The separate **CentralMontereyBay_SBETellipsoid_sxs_ancillary.tar.gz** has SHA-256 `1412f802dece3a063aa319372cb6ab478f1ab97164c2ff86a20e06ee75fbddc7` and 31 SeaSwathplus `.sxs` setup files. Some names refer to 2009, but these do not establish the selected tide or output datum for the five *Reson 7125* GSF files. The much larger cruise ancillary archives have not been inventoried, so these small-bundle findings do **not** prove the required records are unavailable.

Request these exact records for processed file IDs **4181191, 4181192, 4181196, 4181220 and 4181221** (line stems `20091103_191445`, `20091103_193335`, `20091103_210239`, `20091105_200442`, `20091105_211608`):

1. The selected tide or ellipsoid-reduction model, correction files and zone assignment for each line; the **actual output vertical datum**, reference epoch, sign convention and units of the processed GSF depths, plus any subsequent grid conversion.
2. The source horizontal frame/realization and epoch, vessel offset and heading calibration, navigation/attitude quality review and registration residuals across overlapping lines.
3. The original per-beam and per-cell total propagated uncertainty method, surface or CUBE/BASE/BAG uncertainty layer, independent check-line residuals and a defensible *upper* depth-error rule for the released grid, including interpolation and sound-speed effects.
4. The native bathymetry/backscatter surface and source-line coverage at the two research outlines, with no-data/interpolation mask and processing log. We need an auditable source-to-cell join, not only a shaded relief image.
5. A crosswalk from these files to the NOAA 2013 California topo-bathy merge's `point_source_id`, acoustic-source extent, `Full_DataInventory.xlsx` and vertical-accuracy/void products. It must distinguish resampled copies from an independent survey.
6. License and redistribution conditions for derived aggregate research evidence. Original beam coordinates would stay in private review storage unless a separately reviewed target is promoted.

**Acceptance:** reproduce a cell-level conversion to MLLW across a *whole candidate fishing footprint* and its horizontal uncertainty margin, then propagate a conservative upper depth error. Compare native soundings and an independent measured source on the same cells, join independent substrate/fish observations at their true location precision, and screen target, drift and routes against current legal and ENC layers. Until those checks pass, the outlines remain **unranked and unexportable**. A request or a custodian reply cannot itself clear the gate.
