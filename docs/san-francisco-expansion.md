# San Francisco outer-coast rollout record

This is a research and implementation record, **not a released fishing package or legal clearance**. The existing five-coast guide covers Point Arena (38°57.5′ N) to Pigeon Point (37°11′ N) as the San Francisco *ocean* management area. [CDFW's September 1, 2026 regional summary](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Fishing-Map/San-Francisco) says rules can differ inside San Francisco Bay; Bay water requires its own jurisdiction and must not inherit this package.

## Original seafloor evidence now visible

The optional Bodega–Bolinas layer uses original NOAA BAG depth/uncertainty cells and original USGS class-3 hard-seabed grids from surveys H11732, H11733, H11734, H11735, H11738, H12109 and H12111. The bounded review and source hashes are in [`noaa-native-hard-review-summary.json`](../dist/data/noaa-native-hard-review-summary.json), with hazard conclusions in [`noaa-survey-hazards.json`](../catalog/noaa-survey-hazards.json). The 2007–2009 historical grids are not current charts or catch observations. H12109 retained no components. [`build_native_hard_context.py`](../scripts/build_native_hard_context.py) checks the seven review files against the pinned summary, removes overlap, clips fresh complete CDFW MPAs and NOAA GEAs with 100 m planning clearance, and reduces the display perimeter inward. The current output has 133 context outlines; none is a target, ranked area, drift or export item. Rebuilding requires the ignored original review files and fresh MPA/federal snapshots.

On September 24, 2026, a bounded [NOAA ENC Direct](https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/learn/encdirect/) check queried all 18 configured danger layers across harbor, approach and coastal scales around Point Reyes–Tomales. The complete query returned 552 charted features. [`audit_enc_context_overlap.py`](../scripts/audit_enc_context_overlap.py) compared them with the 44 context outlines crossing that bounded area: one H11735 outline lies within the 100 m candidate-review buffer of charted danger features. The [review receipt](../dist/data/point-reyes-tomales-enc-context-review.json) identifies it. The H11735 final office report's historical 53 ft danger sounding is 605.5 m from the nearest returned ENC danger geometry; this does **not** prove that sounding was removed or reconcile its current chart status. The daily source job now collects the same bounded ENC snapshot for review. NOAA explicitly says ENC Direct GIS is not certified for navigation, and the absence of an intersecting feature never clears a route or fishing target.

The complete statewide CDFW MPA source snapshot used for the earlier context screen includes Point Reyes SMR, Point Reyes SMCA, Point Reyes Headlands Special Closure and Point Resistance Rock Special Closure. [CDFW's Point Reyes page](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Point-Reyes) says the SMR prohibits take, the SMCA allows only specified salmon trolling and Dungeness trapping, and entry is restricted in the Headlands Special Closure. These are distinct from the 552 NOAA charted hazards. Exact current geometry, date/method rules and any other access restrictions must be checked again before a site is promoted.

## Official 2026 legal sources to review for a package

The [San Francisco groundfish table (PDF)](https://nrm.dfg.ca.gov/FileHandler.ashx?DocumentID=185056&inline=), updated June 23, 2026, covers the same Point Arena–Pigeon Point latitude band. For recreational boat fishing, it lists nearshore rockfish and shelf/slope rockfish plus lingcod closed January–March and open at all depths April 1–December 31. The statewide RCG combination is 10 fish, with species sublimits; lingcod is two fish, minimum 22 inches total length. Bronzespotted, cowcod, quillback and yelloweye rockfish have no retention. A descending device is required aboard when taking or possessing federal groundfish. [CDFW's statewide groundfish summary](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Groundfish-Summary) also names Cordell Bank as closed to all groundfish fishing. The site's NOAA GEA layer is a conservative planning exclusion, with exact federal boundaries controlled by regulation.

These statements summarize documents reviewed on September 23, 2026; they are **not yet live rule cards**. The official table and overview can change independently, so both are queued for separate daily source watches. A successful retrieval detects changed bytes but cannot approve their meaning. The San Francisco jurisdiction contract now matches its PDF/eCFR normalizers and declares six Bodega–Point Reyes species with **empty legal windows and unconfirmed limits**. The September 24 immutable local packet at `var/rules/sf-2026-09-24-species-review/` passed schema and dependency checks, but 17 of 18 sources failed direct collection with connection resets to CDFW and other hosts; only the federal tuna eCFR source was fetched. Its coverage report marks all six species affected and the registry remains unreviewed. The research browser could read CDFW pages, but that does not supply the collector's original bytes or justify approving their hashes. The separate `Regional legal review packets` workflow collects and retains original documents for Northern, Mendocino and San Francisco on GitHub Actions each Monday and on relevant source-contract changes. Its [first cloud run](https://github.com/Grahammmm/skippercast/actions/runs/36010813817) retrieved all 18 San Francisco, all 18 Mendocino and all 19 Northern watched sources, with individual downloadable artifacts retained for 14 days. That is **source access**, not legal approval. Inspect the packet's current table, gear, in-season, health and area-access sources; then write an explicit hash-bound decision and verify the first regional health feed. Groundfish legal review must handle any YRCA or other method-specific restriction beyond the default GEA screen.

## Ocean and harbor bindings for a first bounded package

The original survey layer reaches from north of Bodega Bay to Bolinas/Point Reyes, so a first package should be smaller than the whole management area. [NWS marine zone PZZ540](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?TextType=2&zoneid=PZZ540) is Point Arena–Point Reyes within 10 nautical miles; [PZZ545](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?TextType=2&zoneid=PZZ545) is Point Reyes–Pigeon Point within 10 nautical miles. [PZZ570](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?TextType=2&zoneid=PZZ570) and [PZZ571](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?TextType=2&zoneid=PZZ571) are the corresponding 10–60 nautical-mile zones. A package crossing Point Reyes needs separate local forecast contexts, not one inherited zone.

[NDBC 46013](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=46013) is offshore Bodega Bay at 38.235° N, 123.317° W; [NDBC 46026](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=46026) is west of San Francisco at 37.750° N, 122.838° W. Each is a reference observation, not a reef-scale measurement. [NOAA station 9415625](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/noaatideannual.html?id=9415625) supplies Bodega Harbor *entrance tide predictions* as a subordinate station; it does not predict entrance current or safe passage. [NOAA station 9415020](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/stationhome.html?id=9415020) is Point Reyes in Drakes Bay and cannot substitute for Bodega Harbor tides. Current harbor notices, entrance conditions, actual transit route and return conditions need separate review. No default trip score or route should be published from these reference stations alone.

## Required promotion sequence

1. Choose one bounded harbor-centered footprint and verify its original NOAA/USGS source cells, historical report hazards and current ENC against each proposed fishing geometry. The display layer is insufficient for target footprints.
2. Finish the San Francisco outer-coast jurisdiction review and species ecology/strategy dossier. Keep San Francisco Bay separate, and add halibut, salmon, crab and tuna only after their own jurisdiction and method review.
3. Check the **full** proposed geometry against fresh CDFW MPAs, federal GEAs and fishery/date-specific restrictions, then establish boat depth, routes and a valid local forecast/observation context. Do not use a point outside an MPA to bless a polygon that overlaps it.
4. Publish a preview first, inspect the mobile map/rules/export failure states, and verify a real daily plus half-hourly regional receipt. Promote only source-qualified sites; retain explicit gaps for unsurveyed water and no-catch evidence.

This record is reusable as the source packet for the `$skippercast-add-region` workflow. It does not turn the three San Francisco discovery sectors into complete fishing coverage.
