# Big Sur outer coast: limited preview

This package covers the outer-coast discovery band from Point Sur toward Big Creek. It has no local harbor and does **not** publish a fishing point, drift, area, route or chartplotter export. Monterey Harbor is a distant planning reference; no feasible transit, fuel reserve or refuge is inferred.

The bounded [official CDFW polygon snapshot](../dist/regions/big-sur-coast/protected-areas.geojson) contains Point Sur SMR/SMCA and Big Creek SMR/SMCA. [CDFW Point Sur](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Point-Sur) and [CDFW Big Creek](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Big-Creek) provide the exact local rule pages; the September 25 Central review pins their normalized source hashes. Both reserves prohibit take. The conservation areas have narrow salmon/albacore exceptions; Big Creek SMR also generally prohibits anchoring or mooring shallower than 10 fathoms, subject to stated exceptions. The app excludes **all four** polygons from automated targets and exports, even where an exception exists.

[NWS PZZ565 and PZZ576](https://www.weather.gov/marine/mtrmz) are the regional marine forecast zones. [NOAA Monterey tide station 9413450](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/noaatidepredictions.html?id=9413450) is a distant tide reference, not a Big Sur current or a harbor clearance. [Cape San Martin buoy 46028](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=46028) is south/offshore; [Monterey buoy 46042](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=46042) is far north. Neither resolves nearshore Point Sur or Big Creek. A regional feed parser now retains the **requested station identity and its own limitation note**, instead of relabeling every tide result as Port San Luis.

The [statewide native-depth review](statewide-buildout.md) found no source cell in its audited Big Sur set passing the app's strict measured 25–200 ft and ≤1 m uncertainty gate. The separate [USGS Point Sur–Point Arguello sediment grid](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2018/1158/data_catalog_PointSurToPointArguello.html) is a 50 m interpreted sediment-thickness context, not chart depth or present-day boulder identification. These gaps mean **unqualified**, not fishless. Before promoting a fishing mark, inspect an original chart-datum grid with uncertainty, original substrate evidence, exclusion and hazard geometry, a safe approach, and a species-specific legal and habitat rationale.

The original [CSUMB Big Sur South Block 13](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/ships/r_v_harold_heath/BSS_Block13_mb.html) archive has now been downloaded, hash-pinned and opened alongside Block 12 in the [native-grid review](../dist/data/csumb-bss-native-source-review.json). Its 5 m bathymetry has 762,998 valid cells, all from -250.00 to -78.32 m NAVD88. Thus **none of Block 13's measured cells is within a 200 ft water-depth ceiling**, even before chart-datum uncertainty is resolved. The companion terrain classes are derived from that same surface, and the original producer access/use constraints remain unresolved. This is a useful exclusion of a misleading large catalog envelope, not evidence that shallower bottom is absent elsewhere along Big Sur.

The package uses the shared region contract and deterministic builders, daily and live refreshes, CDFW geometry verifier, and the draft-rehearsal CI matrix. A changed MPA or legal source is held for review. Roll back the coherent Git/Sites version or withdraw this preview if its source evidence fails; do not substitute neighboring Monterey observations as local Big Sur measurements.
