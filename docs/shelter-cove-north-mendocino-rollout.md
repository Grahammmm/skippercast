# Shelter Cove–Fort Bragg rollout

The `shelter-cove-north-mendocino` package covers the North Mendocino discovery band from 39.45° N to the 40°10′ N management line. It is a **limited preview**: the public selector can show the local forecast, tide reference, source coverage and rules, but no fishing marks, ranks, drift lines or GPX exports are qualified. Its latitude envelope is an organizational aid, not a fishing boundary.

## Original measured bottom and fish observations

- [NOAA H11971](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/nos/H10001-H12000/H11971.html) supplies a 2 m original MLLW BAG with product uncertainty near Bear Landing. The [pinned native-terrain receipt](../dist/data/h11971-bear-landing-native-terrain.json) reviews 13 historical USGS rock/cobble camera windows on two July 31, 2010 transects, with nine visual rockfish codes. They are correlated observations, not 13 reefs, catch reports or present fish locations.
- The [NOAA descriptive report and ENC review](../dist/data/h11971-bear-landing-research-review.json) hold fishing promotion: final office review added a submerged rock, retained charted rocks of uncertain position, and accepted survey holidays and vertical-model discrepancies. The source text does not provide a precise hard-bottom footprint. Exact original HCell geometry, current ENC dangers, route and launch must be reconciled before a targeted position.
- The [source candidate](../catalog/candidates/noaa-h11971-bear-landing-original-camera.json) retains original hashes and footprint limitations. A [pinned source-rights review](../dist/data/h11971-camera-source-rights-review.json) confirms NOAA's CC0 survey dedication and the original USGS camera metadata's no-access-constraint, credit, derived-product sharing and non-navigation terms. The monthly pipeline checks those official pages for changes. This resolves the source-use question, but the camera comparison supports a research priority only; it does not clear a 200 ft fishing polygon or generate a bottom image.

## Local conditions and legal gates

- [CDFW's Mendocino management area](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Fishing-Map/Mendocino) runs from 40°10′ N to Point Arena. A fresh [hash-bound review](../jurisdictions/reviews/2026-09-24-california-mendocino-shelter-cove-all-mpas.json) now covers both this exact rectangle and the neighboring Fort Bragg–Point Arena package. The 18 earlier general sources and the first two new MPA pages had unchanged fingerprints; the additional three local CDFW pages were read and approved separately. The rule card still cannot clear a specific position or future in-season change.
- The repeatable [regional CDFW geometry capture](../scripts/prepare_regional_mpas.py) returned **seven** named features in this envelope: Big Flat, Double Cone Rock, Vizcaino Rock special closure, Ten Mile SMR, Ten Mile Beach SMCA, Ten Mile Estuary SMCA and MacKerricher. Its exact [dated snapshot](../dist/regions/shelter-cove-north-mendocino/protected-areas.geojson) is local to this package and can be compared with the official service before use. [Big Flat](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Big-Flat) and [Double Cone Rock](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Double-Cone-Rock) allow only specified salmon and Dungeness methods for ordinary recreational take; [Ten Mile](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Ten-Mile) has separate reserve, beach and estuary rules; [MacKerricher](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/MacKerricher) permits recreational take under current rules. [Vizcaino Rock](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Vizcaino-Rock) bars entry during its March–August special-closure period. The app conservatively excludes all mapped MPAs from fishing-target and export geometry despite method exceptions. Current federal closures and other local access restrictions remain separate.
- [NWS PZZ455](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?zoneid=PZZ455&TextType=2) and PZZ475 are the coastal and offshore marine zones. [NDBC 46213](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=46213) near Cape Mendocino and [46014](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=46014) near Point Arena are distant wave references on opposite sides of the region, not launch or reef sensors. The first local daily rehearsal retrieved both, NOAA GFS and ECMWF IFS winds, NOAA GFS Wave and ECMWF WAM seas, SST, chlorophyll, tides, NWS alerts, the CDFW geometry and all 23 legal sources. The 6 km HFR request returned **no usable surface-current vectors**, so current and bottom drift remain unavailable. The harbor source matched after its watch used terms actually present on the official page; the page is not live launch clearance.
- [NOAA station 9418024](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/noaatideannual.html?id=9418024) offers Shelter Cove tide predictions. Its [water-level observation history](https://www.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/stations.html?type=Historic) is limited to 2016, so it provides no live water-level, current or launch clearance. The [Humboldt Bay Harbor District](https://humboldtbay.org/shelter-cove-boat-launch) describes a public open-ocean launch, and [CDFW's site summary](https://nrm.dfg.ca.gov/FileHandler.ashx?DocumentID=16182) describes an exposed cove. A 23-foot boat needs a current local launch and return assessment rather than a tide-only inference.

## Promotion sequence

1. Reopen H11971 native BAG, camera positions and original HCell geometry together; construct only measured-cell hard-substrate subsets, explicitly excluding holidays, held dangers and camera-position uncertainty.
2. Screen exact subsets against refreshed complete CDFW MPA and NOAA federal exclusion geometry, current ENC hazards and access routes. Retain no area that crosses a closure or unresolved survey gap.
3. Verify the first publicly scheduled daily and half-hourly regional receipts after publication. Continue treating missing HFR vectors and absent launch clearance as gaps; test stale and failed feed behavior.
4. Only after bottom, legal, safety and current-data gates pass, add reviewed species-specific areas, measured bottom views, rankings and selected-plan exports. Confirm mobile behavior and chartplotter exclusions before changing `status` to `active`.

The [statewide readiness queue](../dist/data/california-atlas-readiness.json) records this preview and its **zero** qualified fishing points. A local conditions package is progress, but cannot substitute for a fishable atlas sector.
