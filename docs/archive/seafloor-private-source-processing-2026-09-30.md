# Reviewed private physical processing

A source may provide adequate native depth evidence while its publication terms
need additional qualification. The old promotion command required government
public-domain rights before any physical reach work, contrary to the rollout's
separation of physical mapping and publication.

`promote-survey --physical-only` now admits a checked native draft to private
processing without changing its license. Matching source bytes, native
resolution, depth datum, valid/shallow pixels and lineage still require review.
`plan --physical-only` schedules those windows; ordinary planning excludes them.

The existing runner and terrain rules are reused. Private-source runs save
complete receipts, recoverable coverage and ranked candidates under
`var/seafloor/private-reaches/<reach>/`, not the public reach cache. They never
update the committed public ledger. Hash-checked R2 recovery accepts these paths
only for their owning reach. Publication rejects a private-source receipt even
with an otherwise current legal snapshot.

Offline tests demonstrate private-only native qualification, invalid receipt
rejection, planner isolation, real masked raster processing, preserved public
outputs and ledger, explicit publication denial, and recovery scope isolation.
The three cached original CSUMB BSS grids were used for local proof; raw archives
and candidates remain private. Public source-rights qualification and spatial
screening are separate outstanding release steps.
