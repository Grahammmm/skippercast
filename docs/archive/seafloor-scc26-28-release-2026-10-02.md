## Source decision

Original archive and normalized depth checksums were reverified locally. The original producer policy was reopened on October 2 Pacific time: public use with acknowledgment is permitted, navigation use is excluded, and express producer permission remains required for for-profit use. The source-use checks reject a for-profit deployment. NOAA hosting does not grant federal public-domain rights.

The independent source-readiness review examined every one of the 38 current direct physical candidates. No source-wide artifact blocker was established in those inspected native panels. SCC26 has four final localized ridge/edge patches; SCC27 has 24 outcrop, ridge and margin patches; SCC28 has ten predominantly coastal-edge patches, including seven with incomplete metric support. Those limitations remain. The producer classes are derived from the same bathymetry and are not independent substrate observations. Their manual exclusions were assigned to the smooth class; they do not establish that measured depth was smoothed or provide a complete acquisition-artifact mask.

Acquisitions remain September 2010, with source-specific dates and NAVD88/GEOID09 retained in the source receipt. Two-meter spacing does not establish two-meter accuracy. Per-cell uncertainty, interpolation masks and independence from overlapping surveys remain unknown.
