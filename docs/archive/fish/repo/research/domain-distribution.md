# Domain, Cloudflare, sponsorship and distribution research

Reviewed October 1, 2026. Recommendations below are product design decisions unless explicitly attributed to a publisher. Domain status and prices can change before checkout.

## Domain decision

Use **SLOFishReport.com** for the first county. Keep the product/repository name `fish`; domain names belong in county configuration. Use one shared codebase and deployment, routing an allowlisted hostname to a county ID. Add counties through configuration, station selections, locally reviewed rules, reporting sources and editorial content rather than copied repositories. MontereyFishReport.com and SantaBarbaraFishReport.com can use the same implementation.

The public [Verisign RDAP record for FishingReport.com](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/fishingreport.com), retrieved directly on October 1, reports registration on May 28, 2004, expiration on May 28, 2030, registrar GoDaddy Online Services Cayman Islands Ltd., and DNX nameservers. This is an existing domain requiring an aftermarket transaction. The live [FishingReport.com sale page](https://fishingreport.com), also retrieved directly, displayed **$75,000** and DNX/ProBroker representation, with Escrow.com mentioned for transactions. The user's approximately $12,000 Namecheap display has not been independently reproduced. No verified cheaper same-domain offer was found.

SLOFishReport.com, MontereyFishReport.com and SantaBarbaraFishReport.com each returned 404 from their public Verisign RDAP endpoints on this date. This indicates no record returned at that moment; only a successful registrar checkout would establish acquisition. Do not describe them as purchased or guarantee a normal registration price.

If the umbrella domain remains desirable, collect dated screenshots or quotes from Namecheap, the domain's own sale page and established marketplaces, compare the full spelling, total acquisition cost, renewal cost and fees, and set a maximum budget. A negotiated offer can be lower only if the seller accepts. Namecheap documents a [Make Offer flow](https://www.namecheap.com/support/knowledgebase/article.aspx/10075/35/what-is-a-make-offer-button-used-for/) through Sedo or DomainAgents; the latter involves an engagement fee. A registrar transfer or ordinary registration discount does not override an existing owner's price. Do not initiate brokerage, send an inquiry or pay fees without an explicit acquisition instruction. Launching the county product need not depend on this purchase.

Keep registration at Namecheap and delegate DNS to Cloudflare. After purchase, add the zone, copy Cloudflare's assigned nameservers into Namecheap, validate DNS, then attach the hostname to the Worker. Authenticate email on a dedicated sending subdomain. Choose a single canonical hostname per county and redirect `www` to it.

## Cloudflare deployment and cost model

Proposed architecture: Worker with static assets for the website and small APIs; scheduled collector Worker; D1 for normalized conditions, report versions, source runs and subscriptions; R2 for immutable raw response snapshots, map exports and generated report/media assets; Queues for independent source jobs and digest deliveries. Keep CPU-intensive bathymetry/sonar processing in SkipperCast's existing offline pipeline and consume its versioned exports. Do not move large GIS processing into a small web request.

| Service | Current published allowances/prices | Proposed use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Workers Standard](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/) | $5/month base; 10M dynamic requests and 30M CPU ms included; above that $0.30/M requests and $0.02/M CPU ms. Ordinary static asset requests are free; Workers Caching has separate request billing behavior. | Frontend, APIs, scheduler and source adapters |
| [D1 paid](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/pricing/) | First 25B rows read/month, 50M rows written/month and 5GB included; thereafter $0.001/M reads, $1/M writes and $0.75/GB-month | Indexed regional/report/consent metadata |
| [R2 Standard](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/) | 10GB-month, 1M Class A and 10M Class B operations/month free; beyond that $0.015/GB-month, $4.50/M Class A and $0.36/M Class B; internet egress free | Source evidence and maps with sensible retention/cache |
| [Queues](https://developers.cloudflare.com/queues/platform/pricing/) | Paid includes 1M operations/month then $0.40/M. Typical successful message costs three operations; retries add operations. Free now exists with 10k operations/day and 24h retention. | Small idempotent collect/build/deliver jobs |

Initial infrastructure may stay around the $5 Workers baseline if shared account usage and workloads fit allowances. This excludes registration, weather subscriptions, map providers, email, AI and any AIS license; it is not a guaranteed total operating budget. Use one county-independent scheduler rather than one Cron per station. [Cron runs in UTC](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/configuration/cron-triggers/), so an hourly trigger should calculate America/Los_Angeles local time and guard a once-per-local-date morning report. Set fetch timeouts, source retry backoff, job uniqueness, dead-letter handling, freshness checks and usage alerts. Source keys, mail keys and tokens remain Worker secrets. Publish a version only after its quality gate; serve the last usable version with visible observation and validity dates when an upstream fails.

## Weather commercial-use gate

The [Open-Meteo pricing page](https://open-meteo.com/en/pricing) explicitly restricts its free hosted API to noncommercial use, with 10k/day and 300k/month caps and no uptime guarantee. A sponsored Fish website should use a paid customer API plan if using Open-Meteo. Paid Standard currently includes 1M monthly calls and Marine API, while history/climate/ensemble access needs Professional or higher. Data is CC BY 4.0 with attribution; that data license does not waive hosted API access conditions. This inspection did not expose a reliable currency price for the plans: record the actual checkout quote before activation. Official government source adapters can reduce this dependence; verify every adapter's rights independently.

Batch county observation points and cache forecast runs server-side. A model grid value for a beach is not a measured visibility observation. Every distribution channel must preserve that distinction and report issue time and forecast validity.

## Opt-in email digest

Recommend a provider adapter with Brevo as the first implementation option; an existing Brevo setup elsewhere does not authorize reusing its audience or sender identity. Its [double opt-in API](https://developers.brevo.com/reference/create-doi-contact) accepts email, list IDs, a confirmation template, redirect URL and previously configured contact attributes. Set county, fishing modes, preferred areas, weekly/daily choice and minimum desired forecast confidence. Retain consent timestamp and wording version, confirmation status and preference-change history. Never send to an unconfirmed contact; apply unsubscribe/bounce/complaint suppression before each delivery.

[Brevo's current plan documentation](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208589409-About-Brevo-s-pricing-plans) lists 300 daily sends on Free and Starter from $9/month. [Free limits](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208580669-FAQs-What-are-the-limits-of-the-Free-plan) mean a campaign larger than 300 does not all deliver that day. Upgrade before promising a same-morning digest to a larger audience. Budget from sends: 1,000 daily subscribers are about 30,000 sends/month, plus confirmations and weekly subscribers. Record actual volume-tier pricing before launch.

Cloudflare now offers [Email Service sending in beta](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/get-started/), so the old claim that it only forwards mail is outdated. [Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/platform/pricing/) includes 3,000 outbound emails/month on Workers Paid, then $0.35/1,000; arbitrary recipients require Paid. Evaluate this for transactional confirmations after confirming beta access, acceptable-use rules and sending readiness. Brevo remains the initial marketing recommendation because campaign operations and contact preferences are more turnkey. Keep the adapter swappable.

Digest jobs use a uniqueness key of county + local report date + preference segment + report version; provider callbacks update delivery status. A provider request timeout is an unknown outcome, so reconcile it before retrying. Queue expiry must not produce yesterday's forecast email. Each email gives the chosen mode's short report, condition caveats, meaningful source age, the full site link and preference/unsubscribe controls.

## Messages and Instagram

[Apple's developer page](https://developer.apple.com/imessage/) describes iMessage extensions and Messages for Business, rather than a general website-to-iMessage broadcast API. [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102053) documents users starting business conversations and receiving follow-ups while a conversation remains active. A recurring fishing broadcast requires its own supported provider/program and policy verification; do not present it as a ready integration. Start with email and native share links; evaluate opt-in SMS or web push later under their actual delivery terms.

Automated Instagram publishing is plausible through the official professional-account API. [Meta's own Postman documentation](https://www.postman.com/meta/instagram/documentation/6yqw8pt/instagram-api?entity=request-23987686-db99ce99-bf76-475c-8b76-718576c11cae) documents the Facebook Login route: a Business/Creator account linked to a Facebook Page, access token and publishing permissions, including `instagram_content_publish`. Personal consumer accounts are unsupported. The newer [Instagram Login documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/) returned a rate-limit error during this audit, so exact newer-flow permissions should be rechecked during implementation; do not mix credentials or requirements from the two login flows.

Build a Thursday weekly draft from the same structured county report: three mode cards, a clearly dated 48–72h outlook, recent catches with actual trip dates and a link to current conditions. Give longer outlooks a lower-confidence label instead of inventing a precise weekend window beyond supported skill. Store media, alt text, caption, report version, planned publication time and source IDs. Initially approve drafts in an editorial queue. Once configured, the publisher should create a media container, verify readiness, publish and store the returned post ID, reconciling unknown outcomes before retrying. Media must be fetchable by Meta using the selected API's documented format/access constraints; confirm those constraints at integration. One county has its own handle, token state and posting record. Posting is a later configured action, not part of this research.

## Sponsorship and audience growth

Proposed first inventory: one clearly labeled local ad card below the report summary, one sponsor card after the map, and one digest ad placement. Sell a fixed county/mode/date interval with a disclosed price and provide aggregate impressions and outgoing clicks. Never give paying charters a higher habitat ranking, better fishing-condition grade, unverified catch attribution or subscriber addresses. The [FTC's native advertising guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/native-advertising-guide-businesses) supports clear, prominent disclosure near the advertising. Use `Advertisement` on promotional cards, retaining labels in email and social reuse. Sponsored links should carry `rel="sponsored"`.

Price pilots after audience measurement and operator conversations; do not fabricate a market rate. Publish an editorial methods/corrections page and a commercial relationship policy. Let local shops and charters submit factual catch/access updates for review with source and observation dates. Commercial crews can read the relevant weather/sea-temperature/harbor information, but the recreational species and regulations engine should not imply commercial fishing authorization.

Growth plan: fast indexable county home page, useful area pages for Morro Bay/Port San Luis/Cambria/Pismo, mode-specific sections, dated report archives, and locally researched seasonal/access guides. Add Search Console, a sitemap and aggregate acquisition/retention measurement after domain setup. Each county needs unique station coverage, species seasons, reports and local editorial review. [Google recommends original, clearly sourced helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) and its [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) address doorway pages and scaled content abuse. County expansion should provide genuinely useful local reports. Weekly social drafts, permissioned partner QR links and preference-specific digests can bring users back without flooding them.

## Activation sequence

1. User acquires SLOFishReport.com at a verified checkout total; configure Cloudflare DNS and sender authentication.
2. Finish live-source/freshness gates and source attribution; prototype uses sample labels until then.
3. Attach county hostname and publish the reviewed site when authorized; retain versioned rollbacks.
4. Configure a dedicated opt-in mail audience, confirmation flow and delivery callbacks; test with designated addresses before routine sending.
5. Connect the county professional Instagram account and supported Meta login flow; begin with editorially reviewed weekly drafts.
6. Measure four weeks of returning visitors, usable report freshness and confirmed subscriber retention before pitching sponsorship or adding a county.
