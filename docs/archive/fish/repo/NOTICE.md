# Data and asset notices

Fish is an independent implementation. It reuses authorized reviewed SkipperCast **data exports/caches**, not copied SkipperCast application code. Public access and private repository status do not grant blanket source or application-code licenses. Retain original producer attribution and contributor-specific rights for every new county/source.

## Geographic and physical data

- **Natural Earth:** public-domain 1:10,000,000 overview land/shore context. [Terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/); original source/hash/process in `public/data/basemap-provenance.json`. Generalized geometry is not surveyed bottom, current shoreline or navigation.
- **NOAA National Geodetic Survey CUSP:** bounded contextual shoreline extract in `public/data/slo-shoreline.geojson`. Retained local creator is NOAA; original 1994/2010 dates, accuracy/citations and unavailable-tile limitations are recorded in [product/source research](docs/PRODUCT-DESIGN-RESEARCH.md). Display quantization is separate from source accuracy. NOAA generally public-domain policy does not cover unreviewed outside contributors in other regions. [NOAA policy](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/about/faq.html).
- **NOAA NOS Office of Response and Restoration:** Central California ESI 2006 fine/medium sandy-beach geometry, retained original vertices and source year. Source item states no use constraints beyond mapping limitations and encourages attribution. ESI oil-sensitivity rank is not a fishing score. Original source/item/terms appear in `public/data/slo-shore-habitat.geojson` and [shore model](docs/SPECIES-OPPORTUNITY.md).
- **California Coastal Commission:** separate state access inventory; only factual approach names/IDs/coordinates are reused, with original links/terms and representational limitations retained. No photographs or full editorial descriptions are copied. NOAA's federal public-domain policy does not supply a blanket license for this separate source, and an inventory point does not certify current access.
- **USGS / USDA NAIP:** NAIP-only natural-color dated aerial mosaic, requested from the fixed reviewed USGS service with attribution. This is historical land/shore imagery, not live satellite weather, underwater photography or bottom measurement. Mixed USGS imagery services with unreviewed third-party restrictions are not substituted. [USGS copyrights/credits](https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits).
- **USGS native bathymetry:** original Offshore Morro Bay, Point Buchon and Point Estero public-domain releases; acquired 2008 and delivered through reviewed native COG caches. Credit **USGS; CSUMB Seafloor Mapping Lab; University of California Center for Integrated Spatial Research**. Original ZIP/COG hashes, URLs, rights, datum/resolution qualifications and rendering changes appear in `public/data/slo-bathymetry.json` and [bottom overlay](docs/BATHYMETRY-OVERLAY.md). Colors/qualified relief are modified display encodings with transparent gaps; no exact-depth/navigation claim. Collaborating CSUMB credit in these USGS originals is distinct from excluded standalone commercial-restricted CSUMB products.
- **SkipperCast / USGS habitat:** reviewed government-only original bathymetry/seafloor-character contributors. Source hashes, native survey dates, whole geometry, nominal datum, rights and publication screens are in `public/data/skippercast-manifest.json` and [bridge](docs/SKIPPERCAST-BRIDGE.md). Ordinal physical fit is not fish presence, probability or current legal eligibility. Restricted/unreviewed contributors are excluded.
- **California Department of Fish and Wildlife, Marine Region GIS Lab:** DS582 protected-area boundaries, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Full source geometry is selected geographically without simplification; boundaries are display context, not current fishing clearance or legal authority. [Metadata](https://filelib.wildlife.ca.gov/public/BDB/GIS/BIOS/metadata/DS0582.html).

## Weather, ocean and observation data

- **NOAA/NWS/NDBC/CO-OPS:** public government forecasts, alerts, measured buoy observations, standard-met historical archives and tide predictions. Original measurement/issue/valid times, station identity and units are retained. [NWS policy](https://www.weather.gov/disclaimer/) requires accurate attribution without official endorsement; third-party exceptions still require review. Tide predictions are not entrance currents. Historical bins/quantiles are Fish aggregations, with missing coverage retained.
- **NOAA / IOOS WCOFS and HF radar, via public SkipperCast intelligence export:** real surface model/observation frames, native resolution/sample stride, acquisition/issue/validity and attribution retained in `public/data/slo-ocean.json`. These coarse fields do not resolve a harbor bar, reef or dive entry. Arrow motion is illustrative, not measured transport trajectories. See [source research](docs/PRODUCT-DESIGN-RESEARCH.md).
- **NOAA GOES / nowCOAST:** longwave infrared WMS imagery pinned to actual acquisition times. Government source attribution remains visible. Brightness imagery is not forecast cloud percentage, dive visibility or rain clearance; NWS sky-cover forecasts are separate data.
- **CDIP / Scripps Institution of Oceanography / UC San Diego:** nearshore model data and original station measurement provenance; reviewed metadata permits unrestricted data redistribution. Credit the producer, retain native clocks and quality masks, and do not equate modeled significant height with actual breakers. No logos/station photos are copied.
- **NASA JPL / NASA MEaSUREs:** MUR sea-surface-temperature analysis via NOAA CoastWatch. Native/sample spacing, ocean mask, reference time and estimated analysis error retained. It is not independent buoy measurement at every grid cell, bottom temperature or a future forecast.
- **SLO County Public Health:** concise factual beach-health statuses and original links/attribution. The public feed lacks sample dates; none is invented. No dashboard design/photos are reused.
- **CDFW / California State Parks shore guidance:** concise paraphrased regional tactics, seasonal context and official access references, linked in the shore-model evidence. Guidance is not measured local fish abundance or universal safe thresholds; identify species and recheck current method/location rules.
- **SoCal Fish Reports / SkipperCast linked catch facts:** a small set of factual dated boat-trip counts/labels is imported from the public Morro/Avila feed under the existing `landing-reports` facts-only source review dated September 21, 2026, pinned at [catalog commit a481da68923649db6fda277af4bd6f40813f6436](https://github.com/Grahammmm/skippercast/blob/a481da68923649db6fda277af4bd6f40813f6436/catalog/sources.json). Original publisher/date links, independent publisher-fetch clocks and source credit remain attached. Creative prose, photos, complete publisher pages, licensed bulk operator feeds and AIS are not reused by this binding. Wider systematic/bulk redistribution needs its own terms/permission review; this limited scope is not a blanket publisher-data license. See [reviewed catch evidence](research/reviewed-catch-binding.md) and [charter pipeline](docs/CHARTER-INTELLIGENCE.md). Reported counts do not imply retained catch, precise location, abundance or probability.
