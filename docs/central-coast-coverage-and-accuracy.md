# Central Coast fishing-map coverage and accuracy program

Scope: Monterey Bay to Point Conception, September 26, 2026. The boat planning ceiling is 300 ft. **Only 132 previously published Morro Bay–Avila candidates have passed the source-depth screen, and those are at or under 200 ft. No new 200–300 ft coordinates have qualified.** The [coverage ledger](../dist/data/central-coverage-ledger-v1.json) counts 579 research-only habitat outlines across six other packages. On the map, optional orange regional envelopes show where qualification is missing; an envelope is a browsing area, not a surveyed reef or fishing polygon.

## Where to look next

1. **Monterey Bay and peninsula:** Use the [USGS California Seafloor Mapping Program catalog](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/pcmsc/science/california-seafloor-mapping-program) and DS 781 original bathymetry, seafloor-character and [video observations](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/pcmsc/science/california-seafloor-mapping-program-data-collection). The paired Monterey products are already opened, but a production depth patch needs a reviewed VDatum conversion and total uncertainty. Independently inspect local [NOAA original BAG surveys](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/nos-hydrographic-survey), rather than assuming the USGS NAVD88 grid can be treated as MLLW.
2. **Big Sur through San Simeon:** Search NCEI's [Bathymetric Data Viewer](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/maps/bathymetry/) for additional original local multibeam surveys and request/access any finer data absent from the catalog. The current broad NOAA catalog intersections yield only 8–128 m named MLLW grids in much of Big Sur. Variable-resolution H13151/W00479 filenames looked promising, but the inspected native envelopes had coarse overview grids and no fine refinement cells. These cannot identify individual rock patches. Search USGS map-block and camera records, and retain PMEP polygons only as a coarse discovery layer.
3. **Cambria to Point Conception:** Finish pixel-level survey footprints for other original NOAA BAG links and paired USGS character classes. The already reviewed H11952/H11953 original surveys yielded no extra 200–300 ft hard-bottom components. Check current chart danger, Vandenberg/Diablo access and exact CDFW/NOAA restrictions before promotion.
4. **Compilation cross-check:** NOAA [BlueTopo](https://www.nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/data/bluetopo_specs.html) supplies elevation, uncertainty and contributor layers, but is a NAVD88 compilation with interpolated cells. Its contributor table can locate an upstream survey or DEM; BlueTopo itself does not certify a waypoint or navigation route. The [26 unique Central Coast tile lead receipt](../dist/data/central-bluetopo-upstream-source-leads.json) is now joined to the acquisition queue and refreshed monthly. Its IDs must be checked against actual contributor pixels before seeking the native source.

The [South Big Sur BH44Z5C4 receipt](../dist/data/bluetopo-BH44Z5C4-central-original-source-review.json) finds 6,991 such below-datum pixels, 6,985 attributed to W00479, whose original variable-resolution file was already inspected and lacks a fine native refinement there. These two tiles are bounded tests, not a coast-wide coverage estimate; neither passes original-source qualification.

The [NOAA NCEI multibeam swath-footprint query](../dist/data/noaa-central-multibeam-footprint-leads.json) now runs separately from the existing trackline and NOS BAG searches. It returned 311 distinct footprint feature IDs across six overlapping Central browse boxes; Big Sur's box intersects 126 feature records. Many are old or deepwater transit surveys and the same survey can have multiple versions or intersect several boxes. Its monthly job fails on a changed layer schema, missing count, pagination/truncation or duplicate feature, then publishes only an unpublished discovery receipt for review. Crosswalk its survey IDs to original grid products and small measured coastal footprints before allocating native-download work.

An expanded 300 ft screen of [NOAA W00614's original MLLW BAG](../dist/data/w00614-original-300-pigeon-monterey-review.json) found 141,331 native cells in the nominal 200–300 ft band that also pass the supplied uncertainty and 2 m planning margin. Their eligible supergrid centers span about 37.114–37.163°N near Pigeon Point. They are north of Monterey Bay, outside the current Monterey–Point Conception spot buildout, and have no paired reef or legal/chart review. This is a useful example of why a broad survey/sector intersection must not be reported as a local fishing spot.

The older [USGS Open-File Report 01-179](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2001/0179/) lists four 1998 EM300 5 m grids from Moss Landing to Point Sur, including SGF5G and SUR5G. Those grids may help interpret large terrain features, but they are older and coarser than the present 4 m target-detail gate. More decisively, a [NOAA Monterey DEM source review](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/30515/noaa_30515_DS1.pdf) says their original metadata did not specify a vertical datum and NOAA processed them as assumed mean sea level. We therefore do not treat those archival grids as MLLW depth evidence or a substitute for per-cell uncertainty. The source archive returned HTTP 403 to this worker on this check, so the native files were not audited.

The original NOAA [F00600 0.5 m MLLW BAG](https://data.ngdc.noaa.gov/platforms/ocean/nos/coast/F00001-F02000/F00600/BAG/F00600_MB_50cm_MLLW_1of1.bag) has been inspected cell by cell. Its 345,584 measured depth/uncertainty cells range from 1.04 to 7.185 m below MLLW, entirely shallower than the 25 ft minimum used for these bottom-fishing candidates. Its [300 ft source receipt](../dist/data/f00600-original-300-depth-review.json) records zero eligible cells. F00600 is now removed from the fine-depth acquisition shortlist despite its 0.5 m filename; this says nothing about other species or uses of its shallow harbor survey.

The original [H11951 1 m, 2 m and 4 m MLLW BAGs](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/nos-hydrographic-survey) have also been screened by native cell center. The 1 m and 2 m grids contain no nominal 200–300 ft cells; the 4 m grid has 667,308 such cells that satisfy the depth and uncertainty margin, but **none is north of 34.45°N in the Morro Bay–Point Conception browse sector**. The [three source receipts](../dist/data/h11951-4m-original-300-depth-review.json) are linked in the acquisition queue. The 1 m and 2 m grids do contain shallower measured cells in the sector, so this is a deeper-band geographic refutation, not a claim that H11951 has no useful Central Coast data.

### Source-finding and promotion decision tree

For each uncovered 5–10 nautical mile coastal segment, start with original NOAA NOS survey discovery and USGS CSMP/DS 781 map blocks, then use BlueTopo's contributor IDs to search for **additional original** surveys. Record an explicit no-data result where the catalog is broad or coarse; a survey envelope is not a measured footprint. An original depth grid must supply measured-cell mask, resolution, acquisition date, horizontal realization and epoch, vertical datum and per-cell uncertainty. Pair its cells with independently interpretable substrate and, where available, positioned underwater video or samples. If any field is missing, seek a better original survey or contact the data steward; do not manufacture a missing value from a basemap.

For each small box, the acquisition worker writes one machine-readable outcome: `usable-original`, `coarse-only`, `datum-or-uncertainty-hold`, `rights-hold`, `unavailable`, or `no-measured-cells`. It retains the query bounds, request URL, access time, source survey time, raw checksum, archive/catalog identity and rejection reason. A failed fetch remains visible and retries on the next source audit; it never becomes empty ocean or a zero score. The monthly job can run the deterministic download, checks, joins and change detection on a low-cost worker. Only disputed source interpretation, permission and release decisions need expert review. It must not promote coordinates from an automated catalog hit.

## Improve the spot model without pretending to know fish locations

The current 1–3 score is a **physical rocky-habitat index**. Its fixed weights favor relief and rough cover for lingcod and rough-area extent for rockfish. A 1 requires index ≥0.90 plus ≥70% rough cover; a 2 requires index ≥0.70. This is more selective than the previous distribution but is not statistically calibrated to catch rates. Keep the original terrain score, survey date, resolution and interpretation confidence visible. A broad polygon, bathymetry hillshade or charter route does not increase rank by itself.

To improve biological accuracy, add dated, species-identified video/trawl observations and opt-in trip logs with fishing effort, zero-catch sessions, gear, time, approximate bottom depth, and position accuracy. Use [USGS field observations](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/pcmsc/science/california-seafloor-mapping-program-data-collection) and [RecFIN/CRFS](https://www.recfin.org/about/data-providers/) for regional validation only when spatial precision and denominator support that use. RecFIN's sampling program estimates catch and effort at aggregate scales; it is not a precise reef-coordinate feed. Separate a calibrated fish-presence or catch-rate model from the habitat index, train on earlier seasons/areas, and validate on held-out boats, dates and entire spatial blocks so nearby points do not leak into both training and test. Report error and sample count by species, depth band and sector. If effort or independent observations are absent, show “fish presence unverified.”

For pelagic fish, run a distinct dated **search-area** model using observed SST fronts, chlorophyll quality masks, recent species reports, bait and water clarity where measured, and forecast current/wind drift. Show source age and the change between now and the selected fishing date. A surface current is not a bottom current; an offshore model grid is not a local drift line. Refresh dynamic areas only while the underlying data are valid. Never copy the rocky-reef rank or static coordinates to tuna search areas.

Depth compliance and legality are independent of fish potential. [NOAA VDatum](https://www.vdatum.noaa.gov/) can transform NAVD88 to tidal datums, but [its transformation uncertainty](https://www.vdatum.noaa.gov/docs/est_uncertainties.html) must be added to survey uncertainty and depth margin. Use the current [CDFW MPA GIS](https://filelib.wildlife.ca.gov/public/BDB/GIS/BIOS/metadata/DS0582.html) and regulations plus NOAA federal closures before any map/export release. No derived layer is a navigation chart.

## Routine commands

```sh

python -m unittest tests.test_central_coverage_pipeline -v
```

Routine ingestion should run on a low-cost deterministic worker: source fetch → checksum/metadata receipt → native raster gate → candidate geometry → legal/chart screen → species habitat fit → release review. Use a higher-capability model only for disputed source interpretation or manual scientific review, with its conclusion written into a reviewed receipt. The monthly workflow compares generated artifacts against published versions; changed evidence requires a human-reviewed publication rather than silently changing fishing marks.
