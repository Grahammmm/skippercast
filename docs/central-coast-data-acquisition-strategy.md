# Monterey–Point Conception: data acquisition and spot-quality strategy

Updated September 27, 2026. The planning depth ceiling is 300 ft. The [coverage ledger](../dist/data/central-coverage-ledger-v1.json) currently has **zero chart-depth-qualified fishing spots**, 132 older Morro–Avila **research-only** terrain candidates and 579 other research habitat outlines. The old atlas's MLLW and 200 ft qualification language was withdrawn after the original USGS raster metadata audit found no defensible output datum or product-uncertainty bound. A map coordinate, relief score or historic catch is not an exact fishing recommendation on its own.

Primary discovery routes: [NOAA NCEI hydrographic surveys](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/nos-hydrographic-survey) expose original BAGs, descriptive reports and metadata; modern BAGs include separate depth and uncertainty grids. [USGS CSMP](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/pcmsc/science/california-seafloor-mapping-program) provides regional bathymetry, backscatter, seafloor character and groundtruth leads. [USGS CSMP methods](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/pcmsc/science/california-seafloor-mapping-program-data-collection) explain why inferred relief and camera-observed substrate must remain separate. The [source queue](../dist/data/central-source-acquisition-queue.json) already records inspected products and exact next leads; its catalog intersections must be checked against populated native cells. The [specific Point Buchon record request](point-buchon-source-request.md) and [Point Conception request](point-conception-source-request.md) are prepared but unsent.

NOAA's [U.S. bathymetry coverage and gap analysis](https://iocm.noaa.gov/seabed-2030-bathymetry.html) adds a useful **search-priority** layer. The [checked service receipt](../dist/data/noaa-central-bathymetry-gap-discovery.json) confirms its two approximately 100 m categories and records four exact probes. This can point researchers toward archived original survey files or missing mapping, but its better-mapped color may reflect a survey footprint rather than three soundings in that cell. It contains no depth, datum, native resolution, substrate or uncertainty. The monthly source job will flag a changed service, while no spot rank or export consumes the layer. NOAA's ArcGIS item says its underlying inputs were processed in August 2026; that is a compilation period, not a new local seafloor survey date.

## Repeatable qualification pipeline

1. **Discover and inventory.** Search NOAA/USGS by sector and survey identifier. Store exact file URL, rights, acquisition/processing dates, original bytes/hash, dimensions, CRS, *structured* vertical datum, valid-data mask, cell resolution and named source lineage. A bounding box is not measured coverage.
2. **Screen actual cells.** Decode every relevant original depth/uncertainty cell from 25–300 ft, preserve nodata and variation in variable-resolution BAGs, and reject interpolated or overview-only cells from precise spot creation. Keep a machine-readable receipt for positives **and negatives**.
3. **Resolve chart datum and error.** For a non-MLLW source, establish its exact horizontal realization/epoch and vertical reference, run a full-footprint NOAA VDatum conversion and propagate source, transform, gridding and positional uncertainty. A point probe or reported RMSE is not a worst-case bound. Test the deepest plausible cell across the complete proposed fishing patch against 300 ft.
4. **Pair independent bottom evidence.** Overlay original seafloor character, acoustic backscatter and georeferenced video/samples after a measured horizontal registration check. Label terrain-derived roughness as inference; a hard class does not measure a boulder's size. Preserve class confusion and survey age.
5. **Add species evidence at its own scale.** Link open-reference CDFW/ROV transects or CCFRP effort records only when positions, dates, species, depth, zero-observation effort and method are known. Historic catch density in a broad area supports a regional hypothesis, not a current bite or a precise waypoint. Public charter names and AIS remain a separate, reception-limited layer.
6. **Screen the entire usable geometry.** Check the point, reef polygon, drift line, set-up water and route against fresh CDFW/federal MPAs, groundfish depth/season/method rules, Diablo/Vandenberg restrictions, USCG notices and NOAA ENC dangers. A dated boundary check is not entrance or passage clearance.
7. **Rank only supported habitat.** Use transparent 1–3 **terrain fit** thresholds by species, then show source age, resolution, datum, upper depth bound, substrate support, independent fish evidence and missing gates separately. Withhold a rank when its inputs are missing. Do not call terrain fit catch probability.
8. **Validate prospectively.** Save consenting trip observations including unsuccessful drifts, fish/gear/effort, sounder depth, GPS accuracy, boat control and observed current. Compare rank-1/2/3 outcomes on later, spatially separate trips. Report coverage, uncertainty and abstentions, rather than optimizing a formula on the same records used to create it.
9. **Publish research and fishing layers separately.** New files go through a reproducible receipt and human review before a fishing marker or GPX export. A source change cannot silently promote a research polygon. Revoke stale claims when a datum, closure or license audit fails.
10. **Refresh by source cadence.** Run monthly catalog/source checks; recheck legal and access sources before trip-date use; refresh dynamic ocean data at their provider cadence. Record source issue time, retrieval time and failing endpoint separately. Alert on lost coverage, schema or hash changes; preserve the last reviewed data as dated context only.

The near-term highest-value acquisition is the original output-datum/TPU record for the legacy Morro–Avila rasters. That can correct 132 existing coordinates at once if it is sufficiently specific. The second is the Point Conception line-level horizontal registration and independent substrate/video support for the two small 4 m patches. For Big Sur, the key task is **finding fine populated source cells**; more broad polygons would increase map density without increasing fishing accuracy.
