# Monterey–Point Conception: data acquisition and spot-quality strategy

Updated September 27, 2026. The planning depth ceiling is 300 ft. The [coverage ledger](../dist/data/central-coverage-ledger-v1.json) currently has **zero chart-depth-qualified fishing spots**, 132 older Morro–Avila **research-only** terrain candidates and 579 other research habitat outlines. The old atlas's MLLW and 200 ft qualification language was withdrawn after the original USGS raster metadata audit found no defensible output datum or product-uncertainty bound. A map coordinate, relief score or historic catch is not an exact fishing recommendation on its own.

Existing class polygons are research context. |

Primary discovery routes: [NOAA NCEI hydrographic surveys](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/nos-hydrographic-survey) expose original BAGs, descriptive reports and metadata; modern BAGs include separate depth and uncertainty grids. [USGS CSMP](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/pcmsc/science/california-seafloor-mapping-program) provides regional bathymetry, backscatter, seafloor character and groundtruth leads. [USGS CSMP methods](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/pcmsc/science/california-seafloor-mapping-program-data-collection) explain why inferred relief and camera-observed substrate must remain separate. The [source queue](../dist/data/central-source-acquisition-queue.json) already records inspected products and exact next leads; its catalog intersections must be checked against populated native cells. The [Estero 2012](estero-2012-source-request.md), [Point Buchon](point-buchon-source-request.md) and [Point Conception](point-conception-source-request.md) record requests are prepared but unsent.

NOAA's [U.S. bathymetry coverage and gap analysis](https://iocm.noaa.gov/seabed-2030-bathymetry.html) adds a useful **search-priority** layer. The [checked service receipt](../dist/data/noaa-central-bathymetry-gap-discovery.json) confirms its two approximately 100 m categories and records four exact probes. This can point researchers toward archived original survey files or missing mapping, but its better-mapped color may reflect a survey footprint rather than three soundings in that cell. It contains no depth, datum, native resolution, substrate or uncertainty. The monthly source job will flag a changed service, while no spot rank or export consumes the layer. NOAA's ArcGIS item says its underlying inputs were processed in August 2026; that is a compilation period, not a new local seafloor survey date.

A subsequent [original Big Creek/Lopez Point audit](../dist/data/bigcreek-lopez-original-300-research.json) found a sharper Big Sur acquisition lead. NOAA NCEI's hash-pinned 2009–2010 [CSUMB archive](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/ships/ventresca/BigCreek_mb.html) contains paired 2 m and 5 m processed depth/terrain grids. In two non-overlapping **nominal NAVD88** bands from 200 to 300 ft, Lopez Point has **36,806** 2 m and **63** 5 m cells classified as rough outside the current [CDFW Big Creek SMCA/SMR](https://services2.arcgis.com/Uq9r85Potqm3MfRV/arcgis/rest/services/biosds582_fpu/FeatureServer/0) plus a 75 m review buffer. Big Creek South has no rough cells outside that buffer in those bands; Big Creek North has 85 shallow-tier cells outside it. These are populated, interpolated **DEM output cells**, not a count of independent soundings, verified rock, fish or legal fishing positions. The rough class is derived from the same terrain. Before any rank, obtain the CUBE source uncertainty/lineage and a full-grid NAVD88-to-MLLW correction, independent camera or sample support, source-specific public-use permission, and full current access/chart screening. CSUMB's [library policy](https://csumb.edu/undersea/sfml-data-library/) does not authorize for-profit use without express permission, and the original product metadata leave use constraints to producer review.

## Repeatable qualification pipeline

1. **Discover and inventory.** Search NOAA/USGS by sector and survey identifier. Store exact file URL, rights, acquisition/processing dates, original bytes/hash, dimensions, CRS, *structured* vertical datum, valid-data mask, cell resolution and named source lineage. A bounding box is not measured coverage.
2. **Screen actual cells.** Decode every relevant original depth/uncertainty cell from 25–300 ft, preserve nodata and variation in variable-resolution BAGs, and reject interpolated or overview-only cells from precise spot creation. Keep a machine-readable receipt for positives **and negatives**.
3. **Resolve chart datum and error.** For a non-MLLW source, establish its exact horizontal realization/epoch and vertical reference, run a full-footprint NOAA VDatum conversion and propagate source, transform, gridding and positional uncertainty. A point probe or reported RMSE is not a worst-case bound. Test the deepest plausible cell across the complete proposed fishing patch against 300 ft.
4. **Pair independent bottom evidence.** Overlay original seafloor character, acoustic backscatter and georeferenced video/samples after a measured horizontal registration check. Label terrain-derived roughness as inference; a hard class does not measure a boulder's size. Preserve class confusion and survey age.
5. **Add species evidence at its own scale.** Link open-reference CDFW/ROV transects or CCFRP effort records only when positions, dates, species, depth, zero-observation effort and method are known. Historic catch density in a broad area supports a regional hypothesis, not a current bite or a precise waypoint. Public charter names and AIS remain a separate, reception-limited layer.
6. **Screen the entire usable geometry.** Check the point, reef polygon, drift line, set-up water and route against fresh CDFW/federal MPAs, groundfish depth/season/method rules, Diablo/Vandenberg restrictions, USCG notices and NOAA ENC dangers. A dated boundary check is not entrance or passage clearance.
7. **Rank only supported habitat.** Use transparent 1–3 **terrain fit** thresholds by species, then show source age, resolution, datum, upper depth bound, substrate support, independent fish evidence and missing gates separately. Withhold a rank when its inputs are missing. Do not call terrain fit catch probability.
8. **Validate prospectively.** Save consenting trip observations including unsuccessful drifts, fish/gear/effort, sounder depth, GPS accuracy, boat control and observed current. Compare rank-1/2/3 outcomes on later, spatially separate trips. Report coverage, uncertainty and abstentions, rather than optimizing a formula on the same records used to create it.
9. **Publish research and fishing layers separately.** New files go through a reproducible receipt and human review before a fishing marker or GPX export. A source change cannot silently promote a research polygon. Revoke stale claims when a datum, closure or license audit fails.
10. **Refresh by source cadence.** Run monthly catalog/source checks; recheck legal and access sources before trip-date use; refresh dynamic ocean data at their provider cadence. Record source issue time, retrieval time and failing endpoint separately. Alert on lost coverage, schema or hash changes; preserve the last reviewed data as dated context only.

The near-term highest-value acquisition is the original output-datum/TPU record for the legacy Morro–Avila raster and the 2012 Estero WGS84 source. It may determine whether any of the 132 older research coordinates survive a full 300 ft chart-depth screen; it does not automatically correct or release them. Point Conception next needs line-level horizontal registration and independent substrate/video support for two small 4 m research patches. Big Sur has some native populated Lopez cells, but still needs source uncertainty, chart-datum conversion and independent bottom evidence. More broad polygons would increase map density without increasing fishing accuracy.

## Search each unresolved gate, not just more maps

The [ten-track qualification plan](central-300ft-source-gap-strategy.md#ten-release-tracks-and-their-gates) is the work queue for the full Monterey–Point Conception coast. Search a source only when it can answer a missing question, and save an explicit negative receipt when it cannot. The first pass should examine the [NOAA NCEI survey archive](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/nos-hydrographic-survey) by exact survey ID for BAG depth and uncertainty, raw/processed projects, descriptive reports, and horizontal/vertical control reports. For local gaps, inspect [USGS CSMP](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/pcmsc/science/california-seafloor-mapping-program) and CSUMB's original native grids, video, and processing records; a later mosaic of the same survey is lineage context rather than a new independent measurement. Pair any non-MLLW raster with its declared reference frame and epoch before using [NOAA VDatum](https://vdatum.noaa.gov/docs/services.html); VDatum's transformation uncertainty is only one component of the depth-error budget.

The Estero case illustrates the search rule. A [fresh NOAA NOS BAG query](../dist/data/estero-noaa-bag-catalog-gap.json) over the exact 60 research-block envelope returned no survey IDs in that catalog. That does not prove there is no survey, but it moves the next effort to the original 2012 USGS/CARIS TPU and coordinate-epoch record. The [direct WGS84/class test](../dist/data/estero-wgs84-2008-character-sensitivity.json) narrows the deeper research area to class-3 cells robust under a 25 m *class-only* sensitivity radius; it does not measure survey registration error, continuous fishable area, or chart depth. A new catalog hit is examined at native valid-cell level before it can change this conclusion.

For bottom confirmation, ask the data custodians for original georeferenced camera/grab observations, negative examples, bottom-camera offsets, positional QC, and use rights. [CDFW's ROV program](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Management/monitoring/ROV) is an observation lead; compare reference-water transects at their actual spatial and effort scale, never move observations inside MPAs into a fishable layer. For outcome calibration, collect opt-in trips with unsuccessful drifts as well as catches, gear, GPS error and effort, then test later trips and sectors held out from rank construction. A/B/C or 1–3 labels should reflect measured habitat fit and validated performance; if the held-out comparison does not beat a depth-plus-substrate baseline, keep the rank withheld.

At promotion time, preserve a release manifest with the exact source hashes, native surveyed-cell footprint, full-footprint MLLW upper-depth clearance, independent bottom-class evidence, position-error bound, species evidence, current MPA and chart review, rights, and date. The [CDFW official MPA GIS](https://filelib.wildlife.ca.gov/public/BDB/GIS/BIOS/metadata/DS0582.html) and current NOAA ENC hazards are rechecked for the complete target, drift, approach and return geometry. A scheduled source refresh only proposes a new receipt: changed bytes, catalog coverage, law, or uncertainty hold the old research conclusion for review instead of silently creating a fishing mark. Measure progress by independently validated patch area and held-out rank skill, not marker count.
