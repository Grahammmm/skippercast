# Crescent City / Del Norte rollout

`regions/crescent-city/region.json` is a **draft** Northern California package. It gives the shared pipeline a stable harbor, footprint, split NWS marine-zone contexts and locally named targets. It is intentionally absent from `dist/regions/index.json`, the site selector and scheduled per-region feeds. The public Northern coast directory remains a discovery guide.

The primary legal geography is the [CDFW Northern Management Area](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Fishing-Map/Northern), from the Oregon line to 40°10′ N. The draft fishing footprint is a smaller Del Norte slice; it must not inherit Central/Southern season cards. The September 23 local legal collection failed direct access to CDFW and its document host with connection resets; only the federal tuna source returned. A September 24 cloud collection retrieved all 19 official Northern sources and is preserved at `var/rules/northern-cloud-review.zip`. The [September 24 hash-bound review](../jurisdictions/reviews/2026-09-24-california-northern.json) approves the present registry for the exact Del Norte draft footprint; the review gate returned no source changes. It does not approve a fishing spot, a future season opening, or harbor clearance.

For the intended rod-and-reel and crab targets, the official review set includes the [Northern regional page](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Fishing-Map/Northern), [Northern groundfish table](https://nrm.dfg.ca.gov/FileHandler.ashx?DocumentID=185055&inline=), [Pacific halibut quota page](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/Pacific-Halibut), [ocean salmon page](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Salmon), [in-season notices](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Inseason), [whale-safe crab declarations](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/Whale-Safe-Fisheries), and the [CDFW health advisories](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Health-Advisories). Area review must distinguish [Pyramid Point SMCA](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Pyramid-Point), [Point St. George Reef Offshore SMCA](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Point-St-George-Reef), and the [Reading Rock MPAs](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Reading-Rock). The app's conservative fishing-target policy excludes MPAs even where a particular method has an exception. Exact MPA polygons, species, method, date and any tribal take exemptions must be checked against the official rules; this draft does not adjudicate them.

The forecast context changes at Point St. George. Southward coastal/offshore samples use [NWS PZZ450/PZZ470](https://www.weather.gov/eka/marine); northward samples use [NWS PZZ356/PZZ376](https://www.weather.gov/mfr/marine). [Crescent City NOAA station 9419750](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/stationhome.html?id=9419750) is a local water-level reference, not a prediction of harbor-bar current. [NDBC 46027](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=46027) is the nearshore St. Georges observation; 46015 off Port Orford is explicitly a distant offshore reference. The [Crescent City Harbor District](https://www.ccharbor.com/) is a notice source, not an automatic entrance clearance. Regional model grid cells, observation receipt times, advisory coverage and the whole outbound-to-return route still need a first checked feed.

The first unpublished GitHub source rehearsal on September 24 returned 64/66 healthy source receipts and reviewed, unchanged legal text. Its harbor fetch succeeded but the page-watch rejected the page because the site's rendered text omitted the expected word “bar”; the watch now anchors on “Crescent City,” “harbor,” and “district.” This is a source identity check, never a live bar clearance. NOAA's 6 km HF-radar metadata endpoint returned HTTP 404 during the same run. Surface current speed remains unavailable until a fresh, geographically populated provider is verified; it must not be set to zero or inferred from tides.

For seabed review, [NOAA F00562](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/nos/F00001-F02000/F00562.html) is held because its original report documents harbor shoaling, uncharted approach rocks and incomplete full-bottom coverage. The original 1.5 m BAG for [NOAA H11981](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/nos/H10001-H12000/H11981.html) spans approximately 41.2844–41.4774° N, entirely south of this draft region's 41.55° N boundary. It belongs to a separate Klamath/Reading Rock review, **not** the Crescent City package. Its overlap elsewhere with a **predicted** hard class is not verified natural reef. Neither survey currently yields a public fishing coordinate or bottom image here.

The repeatable [region BAG-envelope audit](../dist/data/noaa-region-bag-envelope-review.json) checks original downloaded files against exact package bounds. For Crescent City it records 39 intersecting BAG *bounding boxes*, including two held F00562 files, and **zero unheld fine regular-grid local leads** in the 100 MB audit. Most other intersecting boxes are wide variable-resolution overviews or broad coastal-grid files; they do not establish that any native depth cell covers a proposed fishing place. H11981 is correctly absent.

The separate 199.5 MB original [NOAA H12131 MLLW BAG audit](../dist/data/noaa-h12131-native-audit.json) verifies its 2009 survey dates, 1–4 m refinements, product uncertainty and SHA-256. The [original-cell depth review](../dist/data/noaa-h12131-native-depth-review.json) geolocates **264 fine supergrids** by their centers inside the Crescent City package, containing 454,993 measured native cells and 453,524 passing the 25–200 ft/uncertainty *planning* screen. These whole-supergrid counts include possible edge cells outside package bounds. **13,852 of the survey's 14,116 fine supergrids fall outside California's browse-sector envelopes**, so the survey-wide count must never be described as Crescent City coverage.

The repeatable [H12131 MPA footprint review](../dist/data/noaa-h12131-crescent-mpa-review.json) checks those 264 complete fine-grid footprints against the fresh 155-feature CDFW snapshot: **206 are fully inside and 58 intersect Pyramid Point SMCA; zero are clear of all MPAs**. The original [H12131 descriptive report](https://data.ngdc.noaa.gov/platforms/ocean/nos/coast/H12001-H14000/H12131/DR/H12131.pdf) (SHA-256 `c1aaaefb29bc1dfb3168c62042958bfbdc1f7134e8f8c9658b71038c6c07975d`) labels this a Southern Oregon survey from Cape Ferrelo to Winchuck River, reports five historical dangers to navigation, and states no bottom samples were collected. The 264 border-edge grids are therefore **protected-area historical depth context only**, never Crescent City fishing coordinates or bottom illustrations.

The 504.6 MB [H11985 original BAG audit](../dist/data/noaa-h11985-native-audit.json) now supplies a better local depth lead. Its [native-grid review](../dist/data/noaa-h11985-native-depth-review.json) puts 10,448 fine supergrid centers in the Crescent City package; the [complete-footprint MPA review](../dist/data/noaa-h11985-crescent-mpa-review.json) finds 7,615 entirely outside mapped MPAs, 2,703 wholly inside and 130 intersecting an MPA. The depth/uncertainty screen passes most measured cells, but that does **not** classify their seabed. The original [H11985 Pelican Bay survey report](https://data.ngdc.noaa.gov/platforms/ocean/nos/coast/H10001-H12000/H11985/DR/H11985.pdf) (SHA-256 `06634b6f0ed0864ee590277b176ce17f1ce778d8d66f72c7ccbadf1ba4e8026e`) is a scanned 2008 report; its chart features and dangers still require a careful review against the current ENC.

The reusable [USGS video cross-check](../dist/data/noaa-h11985-video-overlap-review.json) compares original 2009 c109nc camera windows to the 7,615 MPA-clear H11985 grid footprints. Of 532 bottom observations outside MPAs in the broad package, 496 do not touch a clear H11985 fine grid. Twenty-eight land in a grid's 25 m edge hold, including **one rock observation**; the eight well inside clear grids are **seven sand and one mud**. These are transect windows with variable camera position accuracy, not an independent catch sample or evidence that unsampled water is soft. They do not justify a rockfish or lingcod hotspot. The separate [H13206 original BAG](../dist/data/noaa-h13206-native-audit.json) has only 32 m refinements in a broad Saint George Reef survey, so it cannot resolve small rock piles under the fine-grid policy.

Move this package through the common workflow, in order:

1. Keep the reviewed Northern rules fresh through the page watches. Re-review a changed source before restoring its status; a scheduled November crab opener still requires the later health and gear declaration.
2. Keep H12131 protected from fishing-target promotion under the complete-footprint MPA screen. Review H11985's original report hazards and find independent hard-substrate evidence within its MPA-clear native cells before any habitat outline. Retain F00562's harbor hold; screen full candidate geometry against current MPAs and applicable federal/state closures. Keep any unsurveyed or protected water empty. Review H11981 separately in its actual Klamath/Reading Rock footprint.
3. Check local marine forecast zones, buoy readings, tide references, advisory and harbor sources in a first regional daily and live receipt. Missing observations stay missing. The unpublished draft rehearsal now runs on relevant pushes and records these source results.
4. Compile the preview, inspect the phone map and date-specific rules, run the full tests and verify no unqualified geometry enters GPX exports. Only then add the package to the Northern coast entry and publish it.

The [statewide Reef Check archive review](../dist/data/reefcheck-statewide-observation-review.json) adds dated shallow-diver context: 29 transects at one Del Norte site label/coordinate and 98 transects at two sites in the next browse band. The archive's 250 m position uncertainty and shallow, partly protected-site design prevent these observations from becoming exact H11985 rockfish or lingcod positions. The draft is useful source and process work. It is **not** a complete Northern atlas or a claim that a fisherman can use a marked Del Norte spot today.
