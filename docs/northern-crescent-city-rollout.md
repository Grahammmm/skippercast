# Crescent City / Del Norte rollout

`regions/crescent-city/region.json` is a **draft** Northern California package. It gives the shared pipeline a stable harbor, footprint, split NWS marine-zone contexts and locally named targets. It is intentionally absent from `dist/regions/index.json`, the site selector and scheduled per-region feeds. The public Northern coast directory remains a discovery guide.

The primary legal geography is the [CDFW Northern Management Area](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Fishing-Map/Northern), from the Oregon line to 40°10′ N. The draft fishing footprint is a smaller Del Norte slice; it must not inherit Central/Southern season cards. `jurisdictions/california-northern.json` and `dist/data/regulations-northern.json` are a **pending review packet**, not approved rules. The September 23 collection at `var/rules/northern-2026-09-23-first/packet.json` failed direct access to CDFW and its document host with connection resets; only the federal tuna source returned. The failures remain recorded locally. No source hash or rule interpretation was approved from that failed collection. A new successful packet and explicit hash-bound decision are required before `status` can become `preview`.

For the intended rod-and-reel and crab targets, the official review set includes the [Northern regional page](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Fishing-Map/Northern), [Northern groundfish table](https://nrm.dfg.ca.gov/FileHandler.ashx?DocumentID=185055&inline=), [Pacific halibut quota page](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/Pacific-Halibut), [ocean salmon page](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Salmon), [in-season notices](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Regulations/Inseason), [whale-safe crab declarations](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/Whale-Safe-Fisheries), and the [CDFW health advisories](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Fishing/Ocean/Health-Advisories). Area review must distinguish [Pyramid Point SMCA](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Pyramid-Point), [Point St. George Reef Offshore SMCA](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Point-St-George-Reef), and the [Reading Rock MPAs](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Reading-Rock). The app's conservative fishing-target policy excludes MPAs even where a particular method has an exception. Exact MPA polygons, species, method, date and any tribal take exemptions must be checked against the official rules; this draft does not adjudicate them.

The forecast context changes at Point St. George. Southward coastal/offshore samples use [NWS PZZ450/PZZ470](https://www.weather.gov/eka/marine); northward samples use [NWS PZZ356/PZZ376](https://www.weather.gov/mfr/marine). [Crescent City NOAA station 9419750](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/stationhome.html?id=9419750) is a local water-level reference, not a prediction of harbor-bar current. [NDBC 46027](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=46027) is the nearshore St. Georges observation; 46015 off Port Orford is explicitly a distant offshore reference. The [Crescent City Harbor District](https://www.ccharbor.com/) is a notice source, not an automatic entrance clearance. Regional model grid cells, observation receipt times, advisory coverage and the whole outbound-to-return route still need a first checked feed.

For seabed review, [NOAA F00562](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/nos/F00001-F02000/F00562.html) is held because its original report documents harbor shoaling, uncharted approach rocks and incomplete full-bottom coverage. [NOAA H11981](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/nos/H10001-H12000/H11981.html) remains a native-depth/source lead south of Crescent City; its original report describes chart corrections and generalized rocky seabed features. Its overlap is with a **predicted** hard class, not verified natural reef. The next pass must inspect the exact original 1–2 m BAG cells, report/HCell objects, substrate classification, survey date, product uncertainty, current chart hazards and full MPA/legal geometry. Neither survey currently yields a public fishing coordinate or bottom image.

Move this package through the common workflow, in order:

1. Re-collect official Northern legal sources from a working route. Read the documents and current in-season declarations, correct any draft rules, add the exact reviewed Del Norte footprint, and apply a decision with each source hash. Do not copy Central approval hashes.
2. Audit H11981's exact native cells and independent substrate evidence; retain F00562's harbor hold. Screen the full candidate geometry against current MPAs and applicable federal/state closures. Keep any unsurveyed or protected water empty.
3. Check local marine forecast zones, buoy readings, tide references, advisory and harbor sources in a first regional daily and live receipt. Missing observations stay missing.
4. Compile the preview, inspect the phone map and date-specific rules, run the full tests and verify no unqualified geometry enters GPX exports. Only then add the package to the Northern coast entry and publish it.

The draft is useful source and process work. It is **not** a complete Northern atlas or a claim that a fisherman can use a marked Del Norte spot today.
