# South Big Sur–San Simeon preview

The package covers the previously unbound coast between the Cambria–San Simeon and Big Sur packages. It is a **preview**, not a set of fishing positions. Its atlas intentionally has no targets, areas, drift lines or chartplotter exports. Morro Bay is shown only as a distant planning origin; the software does not establish a safe trip, local refuge or entrance clearance.

## Bound evidence

- The region has two forecast contexts across the Point Piedras Blancas marine-zone boundary: NWS PZZ565/PZZ576 to the north and PZZ645/PZZ670 to the south. GFS/IFS wind and GFS Wave/WAM sea feeds were collected in the first rehearsal; headland and nearshore conditions remain unresolved by these model grids.
- NOAA Cape San Martin buoy 46028 is the offshore observation. Diablo Canyon 46215 and KSBP are explicitly distant references. NOAA San Simeon station 9412553 supplies subordinate high/low tide predictions, not a current or Morro Bay entrance forecast.
- The [CDFW Piedras Blancas source](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs/Piedras-Blancas) supplies the SMR and SMCA geometry. Both are excluded conservatively from fishing marks. The Central California rules and this local MPA watch were hash-reviewed on 2026-09-25. Rules can change; users must check the official source for the fishing date and method.
- The original [CSUMB Big Sur South Block 13 survey](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/ships/r_v_harold_heath/BSS_Block13_mb.html) was audited at native 5 m resolution. All 762,998 valid bathymetry cells are deeper than the user's 200 ft bottom-fishing limit in its source datum. Its terrain classes are derived from sonar rather than independent rock observations. Source use rights remain unresolved, so neither its grid nor derived fishing marks are published.
- The original [CSUMB Big Sur South Block 01 survey](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/ships/r_v_harold_heath/BSS_Block01_mb.html) was also audited at native 2 m resolution. It has 5,001,340 valid bathymetry cells; 3,773,386 lie between 0 and 200 ft in **source NAVD88 elevation**, and 166,433 of those share an original derived rough-terrain class. Its measured-cell envelope lies north of the mapped Piedras Blancas SMR/SMCA, but this is a source lead, not a fishing polygon. The exported DEM does not provide a reviewed chart-datum conversion or per-cell uncertainty; the rough class is derived from the same bathymetry, producer rights remain unresolved, and current chart hazards and routes are unchecked. All marks remain withheld.
- The original [CSUMB Big Sur South Block 02 survey](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/ships/r_v_harold_heath/BSS_Block02_mb.html) now has a hash-pinned native 2 m audit: 5,021,485 valid depth cells; 3,033,938 within a nominal 200 ft **NAVD88 source-elevation** screen; 17,169 of those share a derived rough-terrain class. These measured cells are not vetted fishable rocks. Datum conversion, per-cell uncertainty, independent substrate, producer rights, chart hazards, routes and current rules still need review. No marks were published.
- Block 02's separately supplied “accuracy assessment” does **not** resolve that independent-substrate gap. Its [original NOAA-hosted archive](https://data.ngdc.noaa.gov/platforms/ocean/ships/harold_heath/BSS_Block02/multibeam/data/version2/products/BSS_Block02_additional_products.tar.gz) reports 100% agreement at 100 random points between visual interpretation of a **bathymetric shaded-relief image** and a **rugosity class from the same bathymetry**. The [hash-pinned method receipt](../research/receipts/bss02-accuracy-provenance.json) records this as a check of internal classification consistency, with no rank effect; it is not video, grab-sample or other independent evidence that the bottom is rock. The monthly source job rechecks the original method and fails for review if it changes.
- The independent [USGS C0212SC camera log](https://pubs.usgs.gov/ds/781/video_observations/data/c0212sc_video_observations.zip) has rock and boulder observations near Block 02, but its 46 positions inside the raster's rectangular bounds fall entirely on **nodata** and none is within 100 m of a valid published depth cell. The [original-grid overlap receipt](../research/receipts/bss02-video-grid-overlap.json) uses both hash-pinned archives, keeps coordinates out of the public fishing layer and rechecks the spatial join monthly. These observations do not validate the Block 02 rough-class cells or support a new rank.
- The same camera cruise **does** nominally overlap **46** populated 2 m cells in adjacent [Block 03](../research/receipts/bss03-video-grid-overlap.json). All 46 fall in a source-NAVD88 elevation of −72.47 to −67.39 m (approximately 221–238 ft). Eleven interpreted windows describe rock or boulder; one codes rockfish and one codes lingcod. Ten of eleven rock/boulder positions lie within 10 m of a derived rough-class cell, but the original camera horizontal accuracy is itself highly variable on the order of 10 m. One camera line provides these correlated observations. This advances Block 03 above Block 02 in the *research acquisition queue*, not to a ranked fishing mark: the native depth still lacks a reviewed MLLW conversion and upper uncertainty, producer rights are unresolved, and legal/chart/route checks are incomplete. The monthly source job rechecks the two original archives and the spatial join.
- A fresh [CDFW/NOAA partial access triage](../research/receipts/bss03-camera-access-triage.json) checked the **entire three private 100 m Block 03 research blocks plus a 100 m margin**. Neither mapped Piedras Blancas MPA, NOAA GEA polygons nor the queried ENC obstruction/awash-rock/wreck layers intersected those margins. The receipt reports zero qualified waypoints: full approaches, drifts and return routes; other legal restrictions; current method/species rules; chart completeness and datum uncertainty remain outside this check. Original camera coordinates and block geometry are withheld from the public package while source rights and navigation gates remain unresolved.
- The [15-point footprint VDatum diagnostic](../research/receipts/bss03-footprint-vdatum-diagnostic.json) returned a −0.021 to −0.020 m modeled offset and 0.097 m transformation uncertainty. The original survey's NAD83 realization and bathymetry TPU remain unverified, so no chart-datum depth surface or 300 ft bound is established. A bounded inspection of the [official 42 GB CARIS archive lead](../research/receipts/bss03-caris-acquisition-lead.json) found a project `WG84_10N` setting, a processing log recording TPU computation, and a TPE binary member header. These do not establish the final ArcInfo raster's horizontal frame or upper depth error. The next step is a targeted survey-custodian extract of frame metadata and decoded uncertainty, followed by a cell-level conversion and review.
- Adjacent [Block 03](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/ships/r_v_harold_heath/BSS_Block03_mb.html) adds 5,630,223 native 2 m depth cells, including 2,732,539 in the nominal 200 ft NAVD88 screen and 62,734 derived rough cells. These remain source evidence only under the same unresolved datum, uncertainty, independent substrate, rights, chart, route and legal gates.
- A [source-policy audit](../research/receipts/csumb-original-source-policy-review.json) now checks CSUMB's current [data-use policy](https://csumb.edu/undersea/sfml-data-library/) against the original [Big Sur South project metadata](https://data.ngdc.noaa.gov/platforms/ocean/ships/harold_heath/BSS_Block01/multibeam/data/version1/metadata/BigSurSouth_BSS_Project.xml). The policy requests attribution, bars for-profit use without express permission and disclaims navigation. The original project metadata still lists its access and use constraints as to be determined by the producer and contractors. Archive access alone therefore does not clear derived-grid publication; the monthly job checks for changes. This is a separate hold from chart-datum, independent substrate and chart review.

## Update and promotion gates

The existing daily and live regional collectors discover this non-draft package; the draft-region rehearsal workflow also checks its source and legal health on changes. The first daily and live local rehearsals on 2026-09-25 completed without source failures. A source fetch does not itself qualify a fishing position. To add one, inspect a shallow original survey grid outside the full MPA geometry, resolve chart datum and rights, verify substrate with independent evidence, and check legal access and chart dangers. Rebuild shared assets and rerun the regional and repository checks before publication.

If a binding or legal review fails, return this package to `draft`, rebuild the region index, and republish the last coherent version. Retain the original dated source receipts; do not relabel old model or observation data as new.
