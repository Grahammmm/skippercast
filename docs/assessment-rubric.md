# Fixed assessment rubric

These are planning judgments made by a person or agent after source review. The shipped collector does not assign these scores. Comfort and fishing conditions are separate integers from 1–10; **overall is the lower score**. Neither score predicts catch success.

| Score | Comfort | Fishing conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | Exceptional whole trip: ordinarily sustained wind ≤6 kt, gusts ≤9 kt, significant seas ≤2 ft, little chop, favorable swell/heading combination, and resolved entrance/return assessment. | Exceptionally stable platform and manageable drift for four actual hours; strong evidence for effective bottom contact over legal structure within 200 ft. |
| 9 | Excellent whole trip: sustained ≤8 kt, gusts ≤12 kt, significant seas ≤3 ft, minimal chop, with swell, travel direction, entrance, and return supporting comfort. Passing numbers alone is insufficient. | Excellent opportunity to fish effectively for at least four hours, with manageable motion/drift and no material unresolved bottom-contact or current constraint. |
| 8 | Good, with a meaningful compromise in wind, seas, chop, heading, or return comfort. Does not qualify. | Fishable, with a meaningful compromise in drift, repositioning, roll, bottom contact, or time. Does not qualify. |
| 7 | Several moderate compromises or one substantial compromise. | Several moderate presentation or time compromises, or one substantial compromise. |
| 1–6 | Materially uncomfortable, poor, or hazardous; record the limiting factor. | Poor ability to fish the proposed rig, structure, depth, or window; record the limiting factor. |
| Unrated | Critical evidence missing or contradictory. | Critical evidence missing or contradictory. |

An opportunity qualifies only when **both scores are at least 9**, numeric limits hold throughout the trip, verification is complete, confidence is Moderate or High, and there are no material hazards or critical gaps.

An applicable advisory overlapping the trip, hazardous entrance, dense fog, thunderstorms, deteriorating return, illegal fishing area, depth-limit failure, or fewer than four actual fishing hours disqualifies the recommendation regardless of scores. Lack of a precise bottom-current measurement must not be replaced with an invented value; assess whether the available evidence leaves a material uncertainty.

Confidence is separate from the score:

- **High:** near-term forecast, strong model agreement, matching observations broadly tracking it, and resolved material local/entrance questions.
- **Moderate:** consistent whole-trip evidence with only limited, noncritical uncertainty.
- **Low / insufficient:** material model disagreement, missing critical fields, stale/unverified timing, or unresolved entrance/return questions.

Dates four to seven days ahead are **provisional**, normally at most Moderate confidence. Missing evidence never defaults to 9. Ordinary patchy fog is not automatically dense fog; determine whether it affects the actual planned window.
