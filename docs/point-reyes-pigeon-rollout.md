# Point Reyes–Pigeon Point source-review preview

This package fills the gap between Bodega–Point Reyes and Pigeon Point–Monterey Bay in the San Francisco coastal directory. The [region contract](../regions/point-reyes-pigeon/region.json) uses Pillar Point Harbor as a planning departure, a 2019 Parker 23 at 20 kt, and a 200 ft bottom-fishing limit. The bounded outer-coast rectangle is a browse and source-query extent, **not** a route, fishing polygon or legal boundary. The package intentionally publishes zero qualified fishing targets, bottom views, drifts and exports.

The first September 25, 2026 daily run fetched 67/67 configured sources. The distinct GFS/IFS wind and GFS Wave/WAM wave products each returned all three regional samples. [NOAA Pillar Point Harbor station 9414131](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/stationhome.html?id=9414131) returned 35 water-level predictions. [NDBC 46012](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=46012) and [46026](https://www.ndbc.noaa.gov/station_page.php?station=46026) returned observations. Station 46012 is currently an uncrewed surface vehicle near a nominal offshore position, with a large watch circle and low sensor height; it is not a fixed shallow-reef or harbor-entrance instrument. Local NOAA ocean tiles populated 312/357 SST, 44/638 chlorophyll and 200/255 6 km surface-current cells. Unpopulated cells remain unavailable; surface flow is not bottom drift.

The [CDFW source snapshot](../dist/regions/point-reyes-pigeon/protected-areas.geojson) contains 14 bounded marine-area features. The state GeoJSON service delivered invalid nested-shell MultiPolygons for North and Southeast Farallon special closures. The regional compiler allows repair **only for those two exact names**, unions every original component so none is dropped or converted to a permissive hole, checks that the valid result covers every original component, and retains the raw response digest. Three San Francisco Bay features also intersect the broad query rectangle; this is an intentionally overinclusive exclusion screen, not an assertion that the bay belongs to the offshore fishing package. A later exact coastal mask may reduce display clutter only after it proves that no relevant closure was omitted.

The San Francisco jurisdiction now watches six additional [CDFW MPA rule pages](https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Marine/MPAs): Duxbury Reef, Double Point, Montara/Pillar Point, Egg Rock, North Farallon and Southeast Farallon. Together with the existing Point Reyes page, the September 25 decision hash-reviewed all 24 jurisdiction sources and the new regional footprint. The rules differ materially: Duxbury finfish permission is shore-only; Montara is no-take; Pillar Point has method-specific exceptions; the Farallon SMCA salmon-trolling exception does not apply to its reserve or special closure. The app conservatively excludes **every mapped MPA and special closure** from target promotion rather than generalizing an exception across species. Exact position, date, method and current access still require the official source.

The [statewide source queue](../dist/data/california-atlas-readiness.json) has 35 NOAA catalog survey leads, 27 fine regular-BAG bounding-box leads, eight substrate-overlap bounding-box leads and nine nearby USGS DS 781 map-area catalogs for this sector. Those are **source leads**, not surveyed fishing grounds. A rectangle can intersect a BAG with no usable native cells or confirmed hard bottom. The next import is an original native-depth and independently classified substrate intersection outside complete MPA and chart-danger geometry, followed by local safe-route and species evidence review. No target is promoted from the source queue alone.

An additional [original NOAA F00789 descriptive-report review](https://data.ngdc.noaa.gov/platforms/ocean/nos/coast/F00001-F02000/F00789/DR/F00789_DR.pdf) found that 4,447,044 preliminarily depth-qualified native cells assigned to this latitude band are actually a Pier 80C / Islais Creek Channel survey inside San Francisco Bay. The survey is now explicitly held from outer-coast fishing promotion in the [survey hold registry](../catalog/noaa-survey-lead-holds.json). The statewide queue retains the raw audit count but separately reports held versus unheld variable-depth files, so a harbor survey cannot inflate the actionable research list.

The [H11965 Farallon Islands report](https://data.ngdc.noaa.gov/platforms/ocean/nos/coast/H10001-H12000/H11965/DR/H11965.pdf) describes steep rock outcrops, irregular-bottom holidays, an approximately 51 m wreck-like feature and no retained bottom samples. Its 953,994 preliminarily depth-qualified cells are also held from fishing promotion until the original cells are checked against exact Farallon reserve/special-closure boundaries, the current chart, independent substrate and safe approaches. Neither the report's rock language nor a wreck image is a fishing recommendation.

The other two preliminary variable-depth files are also held: [F00834 is cataloged by NOAA as Richardson Bay](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/nos/F00001-F02000/F00834.html), and the [W00477 report](https://data.ngdc.noaa.gov/platforms/ocean/nos/coast/W00001-W02000/W00477/DR/W00477_DR.pdf) says part of its southeast sheet limit was not acquired near a charted Navy test-area transducer. The bounded variable-depth audit therefore leaves **zero unheld files** for this outer-coast sector. That does not establish that the sector lacks shallow fish habitat; it shifts the next work to the independently sourced USGS and regular-grid leads and exact native/closure review.

The [H12112 Golden Gate survey report](https://data.ngdc.noaa.gov/platforms/ocean/nos/coast/H12001-H14000/H12112/DR/H12112.pdf) describes moving sand waves, multiple wreck and rock chart discrepancies, and ten rocky-seabed areas in its final review. Its two regular-grid source-overlap leads are now held pending precise original-cell, chart, traffic and access review. This leaves six regular-grid substrate-overlap file leads in the bounded queue; even those remain source leads, not fishing targets.

The shared daily and half-hourly workflows discover this non-draft preview; no region-specific cron is needed. An official source change, failed forecast, stale MPA screen or missing live entrance information remains visible and cannot turn a research polygon into a fishing coordinate. Roll back by republishing the prior coherent Site version or changing this package to draft before the next build.
