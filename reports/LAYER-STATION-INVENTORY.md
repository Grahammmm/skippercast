# Layer and station inventory (LAYER-INVENTORY-1)

G asked (2026-11-07) whether we have an inventory of every buoy and station we need for temperature, chlorophyll and the other layers we could update regularly. This report is that inventory. Its machine-readable twin is `catalog/layer-inventory.json`. It covers the California outer coast in the 19 sectors of `catalog/coastal-sectors.json`. No app code reads either file and nothing in the app changes.

## Summary for G

- **Stations: 41** (23 NDBC/CDIP buoys and 18 NOAA tide stations). That is all 39 in `station-locations.json` plus the 2 alternates `station-failover.json` reviewed and turned down. **33** show up in today's published feeds. **6** are set up in a region but were not in any published feed (tide stations listed under a region context, or as failover backups). **2** are not used.
- **Layers: 19** data products across 15 themes. **14** are in use now. The rest are approved backups (MUR SST, MODIS chlorophyll, the ERDDAP copy of HF radar) or candidates (upwelling index, subsurface models).
- **Gap stretches: 70** places where a variable has no inventory station within 25 nmi of the coast: water temp 8, air temp 15, wind 14, waves 8, currents 19, tides 6. **32** of them have no public station to add at all, so those stretches have to rely on the gridded layers. For the other 38, this report names **46** candidate stations (NDBC, CDIP, CeNCOOS, SCCOOS, CO-OPS) at 45 distinct sites (La Jolla's CO-OPS gauge LJAC1 and the Scripps Pier CDIP station LJPC1 share one pier). None of them is an existing inventory station under another provider's id: 10 NDBC listings that relay our own CO-OPS stations or buoy sites (for example PRYC1 = Point Reyes 9415020) are kept as alternate access paths in Part A, not as additions.
- **Share of the coast within 25 nmi of a station.** Each pair is our stations now -> with every public station added (ESTIMATED): water temp 84% -> 92%; air temp 60% -> 88%; wind 63% -> 84%; waves 81% -> 87%; currents 12% -> 26%; tides 84% -> 99%.
- **Biggest holes.** North Mendocino (Punta Gorda to Westport, about 75 km) has no public water-temperature, wind or wave station within 25 nmi. Neither does the Big Sur to San Simeon stretch for wind and waves. The Klamath to Orick shore has none for waves or wind, and the Ventura shoreline has none for wind. Currents come almost entirely from HF radar and the WCOFS model; very few in-situ current meters exist.
- **Finding (one snapshot, MEASURED).** In the 2026-11-06 daily feed, the HF-radar currents grid was published as *missing* (no usable cells) in 15 of 16 published regions. On the same coast, the hourly intelligence step had 234 of 368 cells for Morro Bay. The daily one-hour snapshot looks like the weak point. This needs a follow-up card, not a fix in this PR.
- **Labels.** MEASURED = read from a provider file, provider metadata or our published feeds on 2026-11-07. ESTIMATED = computed here (distances, coverage, sectors) or taken from catalog text. UNKNOWN = could not be determined.

- 46259's newest NDBC record was 2026-11-07T08:56Z when read at 2026-11-07T14:18:45+00:00; every other buoy had reported within the hour (MEASURED).
- CO-OPS stations listed under a context (for example Arena Cove 9416841 and Point Reyes 9415020) are configured but did not appear in any published feed. The daily collector reads only each region's main `stations.tide`.
- 46015 (Port Orford, OR) is 53 nmi from California and serves Crescent City only as a distant reference. 46086 (San Clemente Basin) is 41 nmi offshore.
- **Provider aliases (10).** These NDBC listings are the same physical station as an inventory record, so they are alternate access paths, not stations to add (identity MEASURED from the listing name; offset ESTIMATED): 1801589 = ndbc:46012 (0.0 km listing offset); SDBC1 = coops:9410170 (0.3 km listing offset); OHBC1 = coops:9410660 (0.09 km listing offset); NTBC1 = coops:9411340 (0.06 km listing offset); PSLC1 = coops:9412110 (0.02 km listing offset); MEYC1 = coops:9413450 (0.48 km listing offset); PRYC1 = coops:9415020 (0.36 km listing offset); ANVC1 = coops:9416841 (0.05 km listing offset); HBYC1 = coops:9418767 (0.03 km listing offset); CECC1 = coops:9419750 (0.05 km listing offset). Coverage uses the inventory coordinates, so these offsets add no coverage. None of the relays reports a variable the inventory record lacks.
- **Currents at 46213, 46236 (MEASURED).** NDBC publishes a realtime2 `.adcp` file with a 1 m surface current for these CDIP buoys. The app does not ingest it: `pipeline/collect.py` schedules only `.txt`/`.spec` and `pipeline.parsers.ndbc` reads no current columns. They count as stations that report currents, not as current data the app uses.
- Other feeds named in region configs: 22 airport weather stations (labels only; no pipeline code reads the airport field), 5 CDIP MOP model sites (Morro Bay), and NWS marine-zone alerts for every region.

## Part C. Coverage gaps by sector

Method (ESTIMATED): A hand-entered outer-coast line of 62 headlands and harbors, densified every 2 km (750 points, about 1442 km). A coast point is covered for a variable when a station reporting it lies within 25 nmi (46.3 km). A gap stretch is a run of uncovered points inside one sector at least 4 km long. Pass 1 uses inventory stations only; pass 2 adds every active NDBC-listed station in the California box (NDBC, CDIP, CeNCOOS, SCCOOS, NERRS, CO-OPS relays), CO-OPS water-level stations and CO-OPS tide-prediction stations. Candidates are pass-2 stations that would cover part of a stretch. Distance alone is not comparable exposure: every candidate needs the exposure review used in catalog/station-failover.json before it serves a region.

## Findings and follow-ups (not fixed here)

1. **HF radar in the daily feed (MEASURED).** On 2026-11-06 the daily collector read one hour (18:00Z) of the 6 km grid and published `currents` as missing in 15 of 16 regions. The intelligence step reads several frames and had usable cells. Worth a card: read the newest well-populated hour, or a short window.
2. **Bound but unused sources.** `mur-sst`, `modis-chlorophyll` and `ioos-hfr` are approved and listed in every region's `source_bindings`, but no code path fetches them. `regions/morro-bay/region.json` coverage text for sea temperature still describes "native 0.01-degree MUR"; the pipeline uses NOAA blended 0.05 degree.
3. **Tide contexts.** Context stations (e.g. Arena Cove 9416841, Point Reyes 9415020) are configured, but the published feeds do not carry them.
4. **Upwelling index.** CUTI/BEUTI are free and cover the whole coast at 1 degree of latitude. They lag about 6 weeks (coverage ended 2026-09-27 on 2026-11-07), so they suit trend context, not daily conditions. Adding them needs a `catalog/sources.json` review (new host).
5. **CDIP MOP beyond SLO.** The same `cdip_nearshore` collector could serve the other sectors that show wave gaps (north Mendocino, Big Sur/San Simeon, Point Arena to Gualala), where no buoy exists.
6. **feed-health.json** has no per-station last-good times. Per-station freshness lives only in each published feed's `last_success_at`.
7. **Point currents are published but not ingested (MEASURED).** NDBC realtime2 `.adcp` files carry 1 m surface currents for CDIP buoys 46213 and 46236 (already configured) and candidates 46268, 46275 and 46277. Using any of them needs a new adapter; the existing NDBC `.txt`/`.spec` path does not carry currents.
