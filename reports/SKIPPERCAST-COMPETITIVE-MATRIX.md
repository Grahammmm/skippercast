# SkipperCast competitive matrix: features, map layers, user needs and what to build

Card COMPETITOR-MATRIX-1 (t_bf284d4a), opus-coder. Research date **2026-11-07**. SkipperCast baseline read at `main` 5fd63730e3 (2026-11-06). Machine-readable twin: [`catalog/competitor-matrix.json`](../catalog/competitor-matrix.json); every table below is generated from it.

## For G: one screen

SkipperCast already does things the 26 products below mostly do not document: it screens every suggested place, leg and export against protected areas, shows native 2 m survey bathymetry and hard-bottom classes with their survey and date, saves rules, MPA outlines and the brief in an offline pack, and says how old every layer is. Its clear gaps against what anglers already get, often free: it never shows **where you are** on the map, it has **no daily per-boat landing counts**, **no tidal current at the bar**, and alerts need a saved trip and an account. In the public reviews we counted, the most common complaint across apps was **paywalls on things users already had**, and the most common specific praise (tied with "everything in one app") was **"shows me where I can legally fish"**. Free California MPA maps are not a moat (FishLegal and CDFW both offer them); the moat is the integrated, dated, whole-plan answer.

**Build next, in order:**

1. **Live position on the Chart** with the protected-area reading for where you are, offline too (medium).
2. **Lead with the offline legal pack** we already have: offer it for every saved trip, show each rule's dates offline, check it is complete before you leave (small).
3. **Route around protected areas** and export the plan as a GPX route (medium).
4. **Daily landing counts per port and boat**, with filed time, once the owner settles the held report-site rights (medium; rights first).
5. **2 m bathymetry and hard-bottom detail beyond Morro Bay**, depth and survey at the cursor (large; some sources rights-held).
6. **Tidal current timing at the harbor entrance** (medium; data availability unverified).
7. **One freshness stamp and one update cadence per layer and rule** in the legend (small).
8. **Simple wind and swell alerts for a home port** without saving a whole trip (small).
9. **Forecast skill and model disagreement in the brief** (small).
10. **One-tap catch or blank entry** from the map with position and conditions attached, private (small).

Do not build: a public catch map or social feed, a bite score, turn-by-turn navigation, passage routing, unsourced hot-spot pins, paywalls on data a user already has, or live charter-fleet AIS hotspots.

Things the feature matrix shows plainly:

- **MPA and closure maps are common, not rare.** FishLegal (free, offline), CDFW (free, online only), Navionics (per the California MPA outreach page) and SatFish ("where available") all document them. What no page read documents is screening a *whole plan* (every spot, leg and export) against them, which SkipperCast does. That absence is UNVERIFIED, not proven.
- **Offline is table stakes for anything used on the water.** Every chart app, SatFish, RipCharts Premium, FishTrack and FishLegal document it; most charge for it. SkipperCast's pack is free and includes rules and closures, but only when the user asks for it.
- **California regulations are thin outside FishLegal, CDFW and Fishbrain.** Fishing Points' rules cover only FL, TX, GA, NC and LA; FishAngler documents land access, not fishing rules; Fishbrain puts regulations in Pro.
- **Daily California landing counts now have a free app.** Fish City documents boat-by-boat counts including Morro Bay and Avila Beach with push alerts. This is new since the 2026-10-31 retro and is the closest direct competitor to SkipperCast's Reports view.
- **Live position is documented by FishLegal, the CDFW map, the chart apps, SatFish and RipCharts; SkipperCast has none on the map.** Its only geolocation use is choosing a nearby port ([web/ports.ts](../web/ports.ts) `locatePort`).

Reading the layer matrix:

- **Satellite ocean layers (SST, chlorophyll, true colour, altimetry) are the paid fishing-map core** (SatFish $129/yr, RipCharts $99-169/yr, FishTrack Premium, FishDope $199/yr). SkipperCast has free observed SST, temperature contrast, fronts with age and uncertainty, chlorophyll and HF-radar currents, so it is at or near parity on the "find the edge" layers; it lacks true colour and altimetry.
- **Bathymetry splits in two.** Chart apps sell fine contours (Navionics SonarChart HD "1 ft (0.5 m)", C-MAP HRB "1-ft level of detail", both paid) without survey source or date on the pages read; satellite-map apps show shaded relief of unstated resolution. SkipperCast shows native 2 m USGS grids with survey year and datum, plus hard-bottom classes, but only around Morro Bay so far. NOAA BlueTopo is a free national compilation updated weekly to monthly.
- **Hard-bottom and habitat is SkipperCast's clearest unique layer.** The only other hardness layer found is Navionics sonar imagery "on select lakes".
- **Tidal currents and weather radar are the common layers SkipperCast lacks.**

10. **MUST-HAVE: One-tap catch or blank entry from the map with position and conditions attached, private by default.** Why: Fishing Points, FishAngler and Fishbrain attach conditions to log entries automatically; SkipperCast's log needs typed positions in the account panel. Effort: **small**. Data: Existing catch-log API, geolocation, the hour's conditions.
11. **NICE-TO-HAVE: Ocean true-colour / water-clarity imagery.** Why: SatFish (1-3x/day), RipCharts (daily), FishTrack and C-MAP Premium document it; SkipperCast has GOES infrared clouds only. Effort: **medium**. Data: NOAA CoastWatch VIIRS true colour (public), cloud masks, pass time.
12. **NICE-TO-HAVE: Upwelling or sea-surface-height layer.** Why: RipCharts (altimetry daily) and SatFish (SSH, SSHA daily) document it; SkipperCast has none. Effort: **medium**. Data: CoastWatch upwelling index or altimetry products; licence check.
13. **NICE-TO-HAVE: Weather radar layer.** Why: Windy, PredictWind Basic, FishAngler VIP, Fishing Points and NOAA nowCOAST (free) show radar; SkipperCast has none. Rain matters less than wind on this coast. Effort: **small**. Data: nowCOAST or MRMS public radar tiles.
14. **NICE-TO-HAVE: Share my trip plan with a contact (user-initiated).** Why: Navionics, C-MAP, Savvy Navvy and PredictWind document live-location or tracking sharing; SkipperCast has no safety or check-in feature. A user-sent share-sheet plan avoids any SkipperCast outbound message. Effort: **small**. Data: Existing share link and trip plan; no tracking server.
15. **NICE-TO-HAVE: Bring a second region to 'active' (Southern California is the plan doc's candidate).** Why: FishDope and 976-TUNA concentrate on Southern California, and Fish City's listing leads with Southern California bluefin and yellowtail; SkipperCast's SoCal package is preview. Region order is the owner's call. Effort: **large**. Data: Per-region rules, forecast, closures and habitat reviews (docs/plans/second-active-region.md).

### DO NOT BUILD

- **A public catch map or social feed.** Fishbrain's sample reviews complain of wrong catch locations (3 of 10), trolls (1) and paywalled catches (2); exposes private spots; conflicts with the no-hotspot rule.
- **A solunar or AI bite score.** 3 of 10 Fishing Points reviews say high-activity days produced nothing; Windy and Fish City explicitly do not offer one; SkipperCast keeps bite potential unknown.
- **Turn-by-turn navigation as a chartplotter replacement.** Position-freeze and pop-up complaints dominate chart-app samples (C-MAP, Navionics); liability; GPX and iNavX handoff already exist.
- **Passage routing with polars and boat-motion models.** PredictWind sells it at $249-$499 for passages; its Offshore app reviews complain of abandonment and clutter; a half-day angler needs the bar and the window.
- **Unsourced hot-spot pins or bought spot lists.** SatFish 6,000+ spots and FishDope hot-bite pins carry no date or provenance on the pages read; the 2026-10-31 retro lists this as a trap.
- **Paywalls on data or features a user already has.** Paywall friction was the most frequent complaint code: 7 mentions across 4 of 10 apps (Navionics, Fishbrain, Fishing Points, PredictWind).
- **Live charter-fleet AIS hotspots.** aisstream is internal-only and MarineCadastre is planning-only in the data-rights register; the classifier has no validation; roadmap forbids hotspots without evidence.
