## Definitions

- `best_tier`: finest tier among grids with CONFIRMED shallow cells in the sector: CSUMB nominal_0_300ft_pixels > 0, NOAA BAG 25-200 ft eligible cells > 0, or a seafloor-ledger reach with a selected native footprint in the 0-100 m band. No minimum area. Grids whose depth band is not recorded (most opened USGS DS781 rasters) do not set the tier; they are listed as finer_grids_depth_unrecorded.
- `tier_uncertain`: true when the tier rests on NOAA VR BAG cells whose spacing is recorded only as 1-4 m, so the true class may be 2 m
- `coverage_pct_at_best_tier`: ESTIMATED reach-level range from the seafloor ledger (low = reaches using only grids at the tier or finer; high = reaches using any such grid) over the provisional BlueTopo 0-100 m band of reaches wholly inside the sector; UNKNOWN elsewhere
- `release_date`: publication/release date of the grid; acquisition_dates is a separate field

## Limits

- Grid spacing is not sounding accuracy; no source here is qualified as an MLLW fishing depth.
- No repo catalog records a 0-300 ft reference area outside the seafloor ledger (Pigeon Point to Point Conception), and the ledger band is 0-100 m (about 0-328 ft), so every coverage share is ESTIMATED or UNKNOWN, never MEASURED.
- The seafloor ledger records one selected native footprint per reach, not per grid, so coverage at a given resolution is a low-high range.
- NOAA counts come from bounded audits (files <= 100 MB); larger or failed files are open items, not zeros.
- Extent figures are per evidence family and are never summed across families; overlapping surveys within a family are not deduplicated.
- This inventory changes no map layer, target, score or export.
